# Jaisalmer Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Jaisalmer → Sam → Khaba Fort → Kuldhara → Jaisalmer**

The circuit starts at the historic city (Jaisalmer) where most accommodation and services are concentrated. Heading west to Sam first lets you experience the iconic dunes before the road gets sandier. Continuing north‑west to Khaba Fort avoids the midday heat on the desert road, and looping back via Kuldhara lets you explore the ghost village on a cooler afternoon. The final return to Jaisalmer minimizes back‑tracking and keeps daily driving under 4 hours.

Driving times:  
- Jaisalmer → Sam (Sam Sand Dunes): 1.5 hours (NH 125, Jaisalmer‑Sam Road)  
- Sam → Khaba Fort: 2 hours (NH 125, then State Highway 16)  
- Khaba Fort → Kuldhara: 1 hour (State Highway 16)  
- Kuldhara → Jaisalmer: 1.5 hours (NH 125)

**Alternative routing:** Enter from Jodhpur via NH 125, bypass Sam and head straight to Khaba Fort, then finish with Kuldhara before returning to Jaisalmer. This saves ~30 minutes but skips the popular dune sunset.

## Jaisalmer City (Fort Area)

**Key places:** Jaisalmer Fort, Patwon Ki Haveli, Salim Singh Ki Haveli, Gadisar Lake, Bada Bagh, Jain Temples (inside fort), Jaisalmer Museum.

### Jaisalmer Fort (Sonar Quila)  
- Entry: ₹100 adult, ₹50 child (3–12 yrs) – free for children under 3.  
- Suitability: 2 km of uneven stone steps; not suitable for toddlers or travellers with limited mobility. Elderly can manage with a walking stick and frequent rests.  
- Best time: Early morning (7:00‑9:00 AM) for cool temperatures and golden light.  
- Seasonal closure: None; open year‑round.

### Patwon Ki Haveli  
- Entry: ₹50 adult, ₹25 child (3–12 yrs).  
- Suitability: 3 km of narrow lanes with occasional steps; toddlers should be carried. Elderly can navigate with a sturdy pair of shoes.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4:00‑5:30 PM) when the sandstone glows pink.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Salim Singh Ki Haveli  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child.  
- Suitability: 200 m of flat courtyard; suitable for toddlers (stroller‑friendly) and elderly. Roof‑top viewing platform has a slight incline.  
- Best time: Mid‑day (11:00‑1:00 PM) for interior light on the intricate jharokhas.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Gadisar Lake  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child.  
- Suitability: Flat promenade (≈1 km) – fully stroller‑friendly, ideal for toddlers and elderly. Boating boats have a 2 kg weight limit per child; infants should be held.  
- Best time: Sunrise (5:30‑7:00 AM) for misty reflections.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but boat rides stop during strong winds (Nov‑Feb).

### Bada Bagh (Garden of Cenotaphs)  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child.  
- Suitability: 1 km of gently sloping paths; suitable for toddlers (carry if needed) and elderly (benches available).  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4:30‑6:00 PM) for sunset silhouettes.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Jain Temples (inside Fort)  
- Entry: Free (donation ₹10 adult, ₹5 child).  
- Suitability: Flat marble floors; fully accessible for toddlers (stroller can be placed) and elderly.  
- Best time: Early morning (8:00‑10:00 AM) to avoid crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Jaisalmer Museum (within Fort)  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child.  
- Suitability: Small indoor galleries; wheelchair‑friendly entrance, suitable for all ages.  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12:00‑2:00 PM) when indoor temperature is stable.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue – “Fort‑side guide overcharging”**  
- Many unlicensed guides charge ₹500‑₹800 for a short 30‑minute tour, quoting inflated entry fees.  
- Recommended approach: Hire a guide only after verifying ARS (Archaeological Survey of India) accreditation at the ticket counter, or use the official audio guide (₹50 adult).  

**Jaisalmer City food:**  
- Signature dishes: Dal Baati Churma, Ker Sangri, Gatte ki Sabzi, Laal Maas (non‑veg).  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft khichdi, plain boiled rice with dal, idli with ghee, and mashed ker sangri (well‑cooked).  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most restaurants are strictly vegetarian, and even non‑veg places serve a full vegetarian menu.

## Sam & Desert Edge

**Key places:** Sam Sand Dunes, Desert Cultural Centre, Desert Safari (camel), Desert Camp (overnight), Desert National Park (periphery).

### Sam Sand Dunes  
- Entry: Free (parking ₹30 car).  
- Suitability: 5 km of soft dunes; not suitable for toddlers (risk of sand burns) or elderly with balance issues.  
- Best time: Sunset (6:00‑7:30 PM) for spectacular colors.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but strong winds (Dec‑Jan) may restrict camel rides.

### Desert Cultural Centre (Sam)  
- Entry: ₹40 adult, ₹20 child.  
- Suitability: Indoor exhibits, wheelchair‑accessible; suitable for toddlers (interactive play area).  
- Best time: Early afternoon (1:00‑3:00 PM) to avoid heat.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Camel Safari (Sam)  
- Entry: ₹800 per person for a 2‑hour ride (includes photo).  
- Suitability: Not recommended for toddlers under 3 (camel height) or elderly with back problems. Children 3‑10 yrs can ride with a child‑safety harness.  
- Best time: Early morning (5:30‑7:00 AM) or sunset for cooler temperatures.  
- Seasonal closure: Monsoon (July‑Sept) – rides halted due to sand‑softening.

### Desert Camp (overnight)  
- Entry: ₹2,200 per person (includes dinner, cultural show).  
- Suitability: Tents have low mattresses; not ideal for toddlers (risk of rolling off) or elderly with joint pain. Some camps offer “luxury tents” with raised beds.  
- Best time: Night of a full moon for stargazing.  
- Seasonal closure: Heavy rains (July‑Sept) – camps close.

**Trust issue – “Safari price bait”**  
- Operators quote ₹500 for a camel ride but add hidden fees for “photo‑session” and “sand‑bag”.  
- Recommended approach: Agree on a flat price before boarding; request a written receipt; verify that the price matches the official Rajasthan Tourism rates (₹800 per person).  

**Sam food:**  
- Signature desert snacks: Pyaaz Pakoda, Mirchi Bajji, Makhan Lassi.  
- Toddler‑friendly: Soft boiled potatoes, plain roti with ghee, sweet lassi (no chili).  
- Vegetarian availability: 100 % vegetarian; most stalls serve only veg fare.

## Nearby Villages & Heritage Sites

**Key places:** Kuldhara Village, Khaba Fort, Lodurva Jain Temple, Bheem Bagh, Mithra Village, Desert National Park (entry gate), Jain Temple at Jaisalmer (outside fort).

### Kuldhara Village (Ghost Town)  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child.  
- Suitability: 1 km of flat, sandy paths; stroller‑friendly, suitable for toddlers and elderly.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4:00‑5:30 PM) when the sun is low and the village glows amber.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but entry may be restricted during sandstorms.

### Khaba Fort (Abandoned Fort)  
- Entry: ₹50 adult, ₹25 child.  
- Suitability: 2 km of uneven, rocky terrain; not suitable for toddlers or elderly with limited mobility.  
- Best time: Early morning (6:00‑8:00 AM) to avoid heat and to capture sunrise.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Lodurva Jain Temple (12th‑century)  
- Entry: Free (donation ₹10 adult).  
- Suitability: Flat marble floor; fully accessible, ideal for toddlers and elderly.  
- Best time: Mid‑morning (10:00‑11:30 AM) when incense is minimal.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Bheem Bagh (Quiet Garden) – *Hidden Gem*  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: 500 m of paved walkways; perfect for strollers and senior walkers.  
- Best time: Early evening (5:30‑7:00 PM) for a gentle breeze.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Mithra Village (Desert community) – *Hidden Gem*  
- Entry: Free (guided by locals, tip ₹100).  
- Suitability: Flat terrain; suitable for toddlers (carry) and elderly (shade huts).  
- Best time: Morning (8:00‑10:00 AM) to interact before heat peaks.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Desert National Park (Gate)  
- Entry: ₹100 adult, ₹50 child.  
- Suitability: 3 km of sand‑track; not recommended for toddlers or elderly without a 4×4 vehicle.  
- Best time: Pre‑dawn (4:30‑6:00 AM) for wildlife spotting.  
- Seasonal closure: Monsoon (July‑Sept).

**Trust issue – “Village guide tip‑inflation”**  
- Some local guides demand a mandatory “tip” of ₹500 per person for entry to Kuldhara, which is actually free.  
- Recommended approach: Insist on the official entry fee receipt; negotiate a modest tip (₹100) only if you enjoyed the guide’s service.

**Nearby Villages food:**  
- Signature dishes: Bajra Roti, Gatte ki Sabzi, Ker Sangri, Moong Dal Halwa.  
- Toddler‑friendly: Soft boiled carrots, plain roti with ghee, mashed dal.  
- Vegetarian availability: Very high; most households are vegetarian.

## General Jaisalmer Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Pleasant 15‑25 °C, all attractions open; desert safaris comfortable.  
- **April – June:** Temperatures rise to 45 °C; early‑morning visits only; some desert camps close for heat.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon brings occasional sandstorms; Desert National Park and camel rides suspended; indoor attractions remain open.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at ₹150 for the first 3 km, then ₹12 per km; negotiate before long trips.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Fixed rates: Jaisalmer ↔ Sam ≈ ₹250; Jaisalmer ↔ Kuldhara ≈ ₹300.  
- **App‑based:** Ola and Uber operate in the city; typical ride Jaisalmer ↔ airport ≈ ₹350.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Rajasthani, heavy on ghee, gram flour, and desert spices.  
- Allergy risks: Coconut oil (used in sweets), dried fenugreek, and occasional use of mustard oil.  
- Vegetarian options: Excellent; most restaurants serve a full veg menu; non‑veg dishes are limited to Laal Maas and chicken tikka.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: **Mahatma Gandhi Hospital, Jaisalmer** (≈ 5 km from the fort). 24‑hour emergency, basic ICU, and a pharmacy.  
- Carry: Personal medication, basic first‑aid kit, and a copy of any chronic condition prescriptions.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- **Jaisalmer Railway Station** (≈ 2 km from city centre).  
- Key trains: *Jaisalmer–Delhi Sarai Rohilla Express* (14601, departs daily) and *Jaisalmer–Jodhpur Express* (14802). Both have AC 2‑tier and sleeper classes; fare for AC 2‑tier ≈ ₹1,200 one‑way.

**By air:**  
- **Jaisalmer Civil Airport (JSA)** – 4 km from town.  
- Airlines: Indigo, SpiceJet, Air India.  
- Approximate one‑way fare: ₹3,500 – ₹5,500 from Delhi; ₹4,000 – ₹6,000 from Mumbai.

**By road:**  
- **NH 125** (Jodhpur‑Jaisalmer) is the main highway; well‑maintained, but sand can reduce speed near the desert fringe.  
- Nearest state bus depot: **Jaisalmer Bus Stand** (≈ 3 km).  
- Inter‑city buses from Jodhpur, Bikaner, and Jaipur run every 2‑3 hours; typical fare to Jaisalmer ≈ ₹600 – ₹900.

## Hidden Gems

**Khaba Fort:**  
- An abandoned 12th‑century fort perched on a hill, offering panoramic desert vistas with almost no crowd.  
- Entry: ₹50 adult, ₹25 child; no formal ticket office – pay the caretaker.  
- How to reach: Drive from Sam via State Highway 16 (2 hours).  
- Best time: Sunrise (6:00‑8:00 AM).  
- Suitability: Rocky climb; not recommended for toddlers or elderly with joint issues.

**Bheem Bagh:**  
- A tranquil garden behind the fort walls, dotted with centuries‑old neem trees and a small pond.  
- Entry: Free.  
- How to reach: Walk from Jaisalmer Fort (10 minutes).  
- Best time: Evening (5:30‑7:00 PM) for a cool breeze.  
- Suitability: Flat paved paths; ideal for strollers and senior walkers.

**Mithra Village:**  
- A living desert hamlet where locals showcase traditional pottery and camel‑milk products.  
- Entry: Free (tip ₹100 optional).  
- How to reach: Hire a local auto from Kuldhara (30 minutes).  
- Best time: Morning (8:00‑10:00 AM) for cultural interaction.  
- Suitability: Flat, shaded lanes; suitable for toddlers (carried) and elderly (shade available).

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide 4G coverage in the city and along NH 125. Signal drops near Sam Sand Dunes and Khaba Fort; carry an offline map and a power bank.

**ATMs:**  
- SBI ATM at **Jaisalmer Fort Market** (near Patwon Ki Haveli).  
- HDFC ATM at **Jaisalmer Bus Stand**.  
- Cash is preferred in desert camps; most shops accept only cash.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at **Jaisalmer Railway Station** or **Jaisalmer Airport**; Jio offers the best coverage in the desert region, followed by Airtel.  
- Bring a passport copy and a passport‑size photo for KYC.
