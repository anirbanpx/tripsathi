# Kerala Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Kochi → Munnar → Thekkady → Alappuzha → Kovalam → Trivandrum**

Starting from Kochi (the state’s major air‑ and rail‑hub) reduces initial travel fatigue because the city lies on the coastal NH 66 and is well‑served by taxis and app‑based rides. The inland leg to Munnar uses the scenic NH 85 (Kochi‑Munnar Highway), allowing a comfortable 4‑hour drive before altitude changes become significant. From Munnar, the NH 220 connects to Thekkady (≈3 h), keeping the journey on well‑maintained roads and avoiding back‑tracking. The backwater stretch from Thekkady to Alappuzha follows the inland SH 19 and then NH 66, a short 2‑hour drive that lets travellers unwind after a wildlife safari. Finally, the coastal drive from Alappuzha to Kovalam via NH 66 (≈3 h) positions you for the final beach leg before a quick 15‑minute city‑center ride into Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) for departure.

Driving times:  
- Kochi → Munnar: 4 h (NH 85)  
- Munnar → Thekkady: 3 h (NH 220)  
- Thekkady → Alappuzha: 2 h (SH 19 + NH 66)  
- Alappuzha → Kovalam: 3 h (NH 66)  

**Alternative routing:** Enter via Trivandrum International Airport, then follow NH 66 northwards to Kovalam → Alappuzha → Thekkady → Munnar → Kochi. This circuit is useful for travellers flying into the southern airport and wanting to finish with a coastal city experience.

## Munnar

**Key places:** Eravikulam National Park, Tea Museum, Mattupetty Dam, Top Station

**Eravikulam National Park:**  
- Entry: ₹300 adult, ₹150 child (3–12 y); free under 3.  
- Suitability: 3 km of uneven, steep trails; **not suitable for toddlers** or travellers with limited mobility. Elderly visitors should use the park’s shuttle service to the viewpoint.  
- Best time: Early morning (7:30–9:30 am) for clear views of the Nilgiri Tahr and sunrise over the hills.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays for maintenance; also closed during heavy monsoon (late June–early July) due to landslide risk.

**Tea Museum:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; free under 3.  
- Suitability: Flat indoor layout; **suitable for toddlers** (stroller‑friendly) and elderly (benches available).  
- Best time: Mid‑day (11 am–2 pm) when guided tours are scheduled.  
- Seasonal closure: None; open year‑round.

**Mattupetty Dam:**  
- Entry: ₹25 adult, free for children under 12.  
- Suitability: Gentle boat rides on the reservoir; **suitable for toddlers** (short 15‑min rides) and elderly (boat has railings).  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4–5 pm) for golden light on the water.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but boat rides may be suspended during heavy rain.

**Trust issue in Munnar:**  
- **Overpriced jeep safaris:** Unlicensed operators often quote ₹2,500 per vehicle for a 2‑hour ride, inflating the market rate (₹1,200 standard).  
- Recommended approach: Book through the Kerala Tourism Development Corporation (KTDC) desk in Munnar town or use verified online platforms; always ask for a printed receipt.

**Munnar food:**  
- Signature dishes: Kerala Sadya (banana leaf feast), Appam with vegetable stew, Kerala-style chicken roast, Puttu with Kadala (black gram) curry.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain boiled rice with dal, mashed banana, and steamed carrots.  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most Sadya items are vegetarian, though fish and meat are served on the same platter for non‑veg diners.

## Thekkady

**Key places:** Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, Spice Plantation, Chellarkovil Viewpoint, Kadathanadan Kalari

**Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary (Boat Safari):**  
- Entry: ₹35 adult, ₹20 child (3–12 y); free under 3.  
- Suitability: 2‑hour boat cruise on Periyar Lake; **suitable for toddlers** (boat is stable) and elderly (seating available). Hiking trails around the sanctuary are **not suitable for toddlers**.  
- Best time: Early morning (6–9 am) for higher chances of spotting elephants.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays; monsoon (July–September) may limit boat operations.

**Spice Plantation Tour:**  
- Entry: ₹150 adult, ₹100 child (3–12 y); free under 3.  
- Suitability: Guided walk through spice gardens; **suitable for toddlers** (short 30‑min walk) and elderly (shade and benches).  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12–2 pm) when spices are most aromatic.  
- Seasonal closure: None; some plantations may be inaccessible during heavy rain.

**Chellarkovil Viewpoint:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; free under 3.  
- Suitability: 1 km steep climb; **not suitable for toddlers** or mobility‑limited elderly.  
- Best time: Sunset (5:30–6:30 pm) for panoramic valley views.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue in Thekkady:**  
- **Fake wildlife guides:** Some “local guides” charge extra for a “private” safari and may misrepresent animal sightings.  
- Recommended approach: Use the official Periyar Tiger Reserve ticket counter or book via reputable tour operators; verify guide ID badges.

**Thekkady food:**  
- Signature dishes: Karimeen (Pearl Spot) Pollichathu, Beef Fry (non‑veg), Avial (mixed veg coconut stew), Prawn Curry.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain rice with lentil dal, mashed sweet potato, and banana slices.  
- Vegetarian availability: Strong; Avial, Thoran (stir‑fried veg), and Sadya provide ample veg options.

## Alappuzha (Backwaters)

**Key places:** Houseboat Cruise, Alappuzha Beach, Krishnapuram Palace, Marari Beach

**Houseboat Cruise (overnight):**  
- Entry: ₹7,000 per night for a 2‑adult boat (includes meals); extra ₹500 per child (3–12 y).  
- Suitability: Stable wooden hull; **suitable for toddlers** (cabin with crib on request) and elderly (handrails on deck).  
- Best time: Sunset departure (6 pm) for tranquil backwater ambience; early morning (7 am) for sunrise over paddy fields.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon (June–August) can cause choppy waters; operators may suspend services.

**Alappuzha Beach:**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Soft sand, gentle slope; **suitable for toddlers** and elderly (benches and shaded areas).  
- Best time: Early morning (6–8 am) for sunrise and cooler temperatures.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Krishnapuram Palace:**  
- Entry: ₹5 adult, ₹3 child; free under 3.  
- Suitability: Flat museum grounds; **suitable for toddlers** (stroller‑friendly) and elderly (ramps).  
- Best time: Mid‑day (11 am–2 pm) when guided tours are available.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Trust issue in Alappuzha:**  
- **Hidden fuel surcharge on houseboats:** Some operators add an undocumented “fuel surcharge” of ₹1,500‑2,000 after the stay.  
- Recommended approach: Insist on a fixed price quote that includes fuel; request a written breakdown before boarding.

**Alappuzha food:**  
- Signature dishes: Karimeen Fry, Fish Moilee, Puttu with Banana, Coconut Chutney.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain rice with dal, mashed banana, and boiled carrots.  
- Vegetarian availability: Good; Puttu, Idiyappam, and vegetable curries are widely served.

## Kochi

**Key places:** Fort Kochi, Chinese Fishing Nets, Mattancherry Palace, Jew Town & Paradesi Synagogue

**Fort Kochi (Chinese Fishing Nets):**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Flat promenade; **suitable for toddlers** (wide walkways) and elderly (seating benches).  
- Best time: Sunset (6–7 pm) for iconic silhouette photos.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Mattancherry Palace (Dutch Palace):**  
- Entry: ₹5 adult, ₹3 child; free under 3.  
- Suitability: Small steps and narrow corridors; **not suitable for toddlers** without a carrier; elderly may need assistance on steps.  
- Best time: Early morning (9–11 am) to avoid crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Fridays.

**Paradesi Synagogue (Jew Town):**  
- Entry: ₹10 adult, ₹5 child; free under 3.  
- Suitability: Flat interior; **suitable for toddlers** (carriage allowed) and elderly (ramps).  
- Best time: Late morning (10 am–12 pm) when guided tours start.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue in Kochi:**  
- **Overpriced souvenir stalls:** Vendors near Fort Kochi often quote inflated prices for “hand‑crafted” items (e.g., ₹1,200 for a simple wooden mask that should be ₹300).  
- Recommended approach: Bargain firmly, compare prices at the nearby Spice Market, and avoid buying on impulse.

**Kochi food:**  
- Signature dishes: Kerala Fish Curry with Appam, Beef Ularthiyathu, Banana Leaf Sadya, Coconut Milk Payasam.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain rice with dal, mashed banana, and boiled sweet potatoes.  
- Vegetarian availability: Very strong; Sadya and vegetable thoran are fully vegetarian.

## Kovalam

**Key places:** Lighthouse Beach, Hawa Beach, Vizhinjam Marine Aquarium

**Lighthouse Beach:**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Gentle slope, well‑maintained promenade; **suitable for toddlers** and elderly (benches, shaded areas).  
- Best time: Early morning (6–8 am) for sunrise; late afternoon for cooler breezes.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Hawa Beach (Golden Beach):**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Slightly steeper entry; **not suitable for toddlers** who cannot navigate uneven sand; elderly can use handrails.  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12–2 pm) when sea is calm.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Vizhinjam Marine Aquarium:**  
- Entry: ₹80 adult, ₹40 child (3–12 y); free under 3.  
- Suitability: Indoor, wheelchair‑friendly; **suitable for toddlers** (touch‑tank) and elderly (seating).  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4–6 pm) to avoid school groups.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Trust issue in Kovalam:**  
- **Beachside rope‑access scams:** Vendors charge ₹500‑₹800 for “safety ropes” to the water, though the beach is naturally safe.  
- Recommended approach: Use the public lifeguard‑provided rope (free) or swim in designated shallow zones; decline vendor offers.

**Kovalam food:**  
- Signature dishes: Karimeen Pollichathu, Coconut Milk Fish Stew, Prawn Fry, Banana Leaf Sadya.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain rice with dal, mashed banana, and boiled carrots.  
- Vegetarian availability: High; vegetable curries and Sadya are fully vegetarian.

## Wayanad

**Key places:** Edakkal Caves, Pookode Lake, Banasura Sagar Dam, Chembra Peak (optional trek)

**Edakkal Caves:**  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (3–12 y); free under 3.  
- Suitability: 1 km steep climb with stone steps; **not suitable for toddlers** or elderly with limited mobility.  
- Best time: Early morning (8–10 am) to avoid heat and crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed during monsoon (July–September) due to slippery rocks.

**Pookode Lake:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, free for children under 12.  
- Suitability: Pedal‑boat rides (15 min) on calm water; **suitable for toddlers** (stable boats) and elderly (handrails).  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4–5 pm) for reflection shots.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Banasura Sagar Dam (Boat Ride):**  
- Entry: Free (boat ride ₹150 adult, ₹80 child).  
- Suitability: Large reservoir with gentle boat rides; **suitable for toddlers** and elderly (boat has railings).  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12–2 pm) when wind is minimal.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue in Wayanad:**  
- **Unlicensed boat operators:** Some informal providers charge ₹300 per ride without safety gear.  
- Recommended approach: Use boats booked through the official Wayanad Tourism office or reputable hotels; verify helmets and life jackets.

**Wayanad food:**  
- Signature dishes: Kerala-style Beef Fry, Fish Curry with Coconut, Puttu with Kadala Curry, Banana Leaf Sadya.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain rice with dal, mashed banana, boiled carrots.  
- Vegetarian availability: Very good; Puttu, Avial, and vegetable thoran are widely served.

## General Kerala Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Pleasant temperatures (20‑30 °C), low humidity; most wildlife sanctuaries and hill stations fully open.  
- **April – June:** Pre‑monsoon heat (30‑35 °C); beaches are ideal, but inland trekking can be uncomfortable.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon; waterfalls at peak flow, but some hill‑top roads (e.g., Munnar‑Top Station) may be closed due to landslides.  

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares in cities (≈₹15 per km). For inter‑city trips, negotiate a flat rate; typical Kochi‑Munnar round‑trip ≈₹4,500.  
- **Auto/rickshaw:** Usually run on a fixed‑rate basis; city rides ≈₹12 per km. Always confirm price before boarding.  
- **App‑based options:** Uber and Ola operate in Kochi, Trivandrum, and Alappuzha; fares are ~10 % higher than local cabs but provide transparent pricing.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Coconut‑rich seafood and spice‑laden dishes; pork is common in coastal areas, while beef is prevalent in the highlands.  
- Allergy risks: Coconut oil, mustard seeds, and tamarind are ubiquitous; travellers with nut allergies should ask for “no coconut” preparations.  
- Vegetarian options: Strong; Sadya, Avial, Thoran, and lentil dishes are fully vegetarian and widely available.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital to major tourist hubs: **KIMS Hospital, Trivandrum** (≈15 km from Kovalam) and **Amrita Hospital, Kochi** (≈10 km from Fort Kochi). Carry a basic medical kit (antiseptic, oral rehydration salts, any personal prescriptions) and a copy of vaccination records.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- **Thiruvananthapuram Central (TVC)** – 5 km from Kovalam; **Ernakulam Junction (ERS)** – 30 km from Kochi.  
- Key trains: **Rajdhani Express (12431)** from New Delhi to TVC (≈₹3,200 adult); **Netravati Express (12627)** from Mumbai to ERS (≈₹2,800 adult). Both stop at Ernakulam and Thiruvananthapuram.

**By air:**  
- **Trivandrum International Airport (TRV)** – 15 km to Kovalam; **Cochin International Airport (COK)** – 30 km to Kochi.  
- Airlines: Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, and GoAir operate daily flights from Delhi, Mumbai, and Bangalore.  
- Approximate one‑way fare: ₹4,500 – ₹9,000 (economy) from major metros.

**By road:**  
- **NH 66** runs the coastal spine from Mumbai to Kanyakumari, passing through Trivandrum, Alappuzha, and Kochi.  
- **NH 85** connects Kochi to Munnar.  
- Nearest state bus depot: **Kochi KSRTC Bus Stand** (inter‑city buses to Munnar, Thekkady, Alappuzha, and Trivandrum).  
- Typical inter‑city bus fare: Kochi‑Munnar ≈₹350; Kochi‑Alappuzha ≈₹150; Alappuzha‑Kovalam ≈₹300.

## Hidden Gems

**Bekal Fort (Kasargod):**  
- Why special: A well‑preserved coastal fort with a 1.5 km sea wall, far less crowded than northern beaches.  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; free under 3.  
- How to reach: Drive north on NH 66 (≈4 h from Mangalore); parking available at the fort.  
- Best time: Early morning (6–8 am) for sunrise over the Arabian Sea.  
- Suitability: Flat promenade; **suitable for toddlers** (stroller‑friendly) and elderly (benches).

**Vagamon (Kottayam district):**  
- Why special: Rolling meadows, tea plantations, and the lesser‑known Meenmutty (Vagamon) Waterfall.  
- Entry: Waterfall entry ₹10 adult, ₹5 child; free under 3.  
- How to reach: From Thekkady, take SH 19 to Vagamon (≈2 h).  
- Best time: Post‑monsoon (October‑December) when waterfall flow is moderate.  
- Suitability: Gentle paths; **suitable for toddlers** and elderly (shade and benches).

**Ponmudi (Thiruvananthapuram district):**  
- Why special: A misty hill station with tea gardens and the historic **Peppara Dam** viewpoint, rarely featured in mainstream itineraries.  
- Entry: Free (no formal fee).  
- How to reach: From Trivandrum, drive NH 66 north to Ponmudi (≈2 h).  
- Best time: Early winter (November‑January) for clear skies.  
- Suitability: Easy walking trails; **suitable for toddlers** (flat garden walks) and elderly (rest spots).

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide 4G coverage in cities (Kochi, Trivandrum, Alappuzha) and most tourist towns. Hill stations (Munnar, Vagamon) have patchy signal; carry a local SIM with good data plan for offline maps.

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs in major towns: SBI and HDFC branches at Kochi (MG Road), Trivandrum (MG Road), and Alappuzha (near the bus depot). Rural ATMs may be limited; keep ₹2,000‑₹5,000 cash for small purchases.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at airport kiosks (Cochin International Airport) or authorized retail stores in city centres. Jio offers the best coverage in Kerala’s coastal belt; Airtel is marginally stronger in the highland regions. Activate within 24 hours; keep passport copy handy for verification.
