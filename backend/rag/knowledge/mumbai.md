# Mumbai Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Mumbai → Lonavala → Pune**

The circuit follows the Mumbai‑Pune Expressway (NH 48), allowing a smooth, high‑speed drive without city traffic. Starting in Mumbai avoids early‑morning congestion on the expressway; a short stop at Lonavala breaks the journey and lets travellers stretch before the final leg to Pune, reducing fatigue.

Driving times:  
- Mumbai → Lonavala: ~2 hours (Mumbai‑Pune Expressway, NH 48)  
- Lonavala → Pune: ~1 hour (NH 48)

**Alternative routing:** Enter via the coastal road (Marine Drive → Bandra → Vasai) and exit northward on the Western Express Highway (NH 48) to avoid the expressway tolls, but expect heavier urban traffic.

## South Mumbai

**Key places:** Gateway of India, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST), Elephanta Caves, Marine Drive, Chowpatty Beach, Dr. Bhau Daji Lad Museum  

### Gateway of India  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Flat promenade, wheelchair‑friendly ramps; **suitable for toddlers and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Early morning (07:00–09:00) for cool breezes and fewer crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon (June–September) may make the waterfront slippery.

### Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus (CST)  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Historic railway station with marble floors; **suitable for all ages**, but steep stairs to the upper gallery may challenge mobility‑limited travellers.  
- Best time of day: Mid‑day (11:00–13:00) when guided tours are available.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Elephanta Caves (Ferry from Gateway)  
- Entry: ₹40/adult, ₹20/child (under 12)  
- Suitability: 2 km boat ride plus 3 km of uneven stone steps; **not suitable for toddlers or elderly with mobility issues**.  
- Best time of day: First ferry (06:30) to avoid heat and crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays and during heavy monsoon (July–August).

### Marine Drive (the “Queen’s Necklace”)  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Wide, paved promenade with benches; **ideal for toddlers in strollers and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Sunset (18:00–19:30) for iconic lighting.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon rain can make the walkway slippery.

### Chowpatty Beach  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Soft sand, gentle slope; **suitable for toddlers (with supervision) and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Evening (20:00–22:00) for street‑food stalls and cooler air.  
- Seasonal closure: None; during monsoon the sea can be rough, so avoid swimming.

### Dr. Bhau Daji Lad Museum  
- Entry: ₹50/adult, ₹20/child (under 12)  
- Suitability: Small galleries on a single floor; **suitable for toddlers (short visits) and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Late morning (10:00–12:00) before the lunch crowd.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for South Mumbai:**  
- **Cab overcharging near tourist spots:** Drivers may claim the meter is broken and quote a flat fare that is 30‑40 % higher.  
- Recommended approach: Insist on the meter, or use app‑based cabs (Uber/Ola) which display the fare upfront; keep a note of the approximate distance (e.g., Gateway to CST ≈ 3 km, ~₹120).

**South Mumbai food:**  
- Signature dishes: Vada Pav, Bombay Sandwich, Bhel Puri, Pav Bhaji, Keema Pao.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft idli, plain rice with dal, plain dhokla (steamed chickpea cake).  
- Vegetarian availability: **Very high**; most street stalls offer veg versions, and many dishes are naturally vegetarian (e.g., Bhel Puri, Pav Bhaji).

## North Mumbai

**Key places:** Haji Ali Dargah, Bandra Bandstand, Juhu Beach, Sanjay Gandhi National Park, Global Vipassana Pagoda, Worli Sea Face  

### Haji Ali Dargah  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child (donations welcome)  
- Suitability: 500 m walk over a causeway with uneven stones; **not suitable for toddlers in strollers or elderly with balance problems**.  
- Best time of day: Early dawn (05:30–07:00) for serene atmosphere and cooler temperatures.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon may flood the causeway.

### Bandra Bandstand  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Flat promenade with benches; **suitable for toddlers and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Late afternoon (16:00–18:00) for sunset views over the Arabian Sea.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Juhu Beach  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Wide sandy stretch; **suitable for toddlers (with supervision) and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Evening (19:00–22:00) for iconic street‑food stalls (Pani Puri, Bhel).  
- Seasonal closure: None; monsoon waves can be strong, so avoid swimming.

### Sanjay Gandhi National Park (including Kanheri Caves)  
- Entry: ₹60/adult, ₹30/child (under 12)  
- Suitability: 6 km of paved trails; **suitable for toddlers in a carrier and elderly who can manage gentle walks**. Kanheri Caves add a 1 km uneven segment (not toddler‑friendly).  
- Best time of day: Early morning (06:00–09:00) to beat heat and see wildlife.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays; heavy monsoon may restrict some trails.

### Global Vipassana Pagoda  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child (donations accepted)  
- Suitability: Large dome with smooth ramps; **suitable for toddlers and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Mid‑day (12:00–14:00) when the interior is bright for photography.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

### Worli Sea Face  
- Entry: ₹0/adult, ₹0/child  
- Suitability: Flat walkway with seating; **ideal for toddlers in strollers and elderly**.  
- Best time of day: Sunset (18:30–20:00) for city‑scape views.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for North Mumbai:**  
- **Unauthorized “guide” at Haji Ali Dargah:** Individuals may charge ₹200–₹500 for a “blessing tour.”  
- Recommended approach: No guide is needed; use the official information board or a reputable guidebook; if you do hire a guide, ensure they are registered with the Maharashtra Tourism Development Corporation (MTDC).

**North Mumbai food:**  
- Signature dishes: Prawn Koli Curry, Bombay‑style Fish Fry, Misal Pav (vegetarian version), Modak, Coconut Ladoo.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft boiled potatoes, plain rice with ghee, mild vegetable khichdi.  
- Vegetarian availability: **Excellent**; most Koli stalls also serve veg options like vegetable fried rice and coconut chutney.

## General Mumbai Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – February:** Pleasant 22‑30 °C, low humidity; most attractions fully open, festivals (Ganesh Chaturthi) increase crowds but add cultural vibrancy.  
- **March – May:** Warm 30‑35 °C; beaches are crowded, but indoor museums remain comfortable.  
- **June – September:** Monsoon (heavy rain, 250 mm +); outdoor sites like Elephanta Caves and beaches may be inaccessible; however, hotel rates drop 20‑30 % and indoor attractions are less crowded.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Base ₹15 + ₹13 per km; meters are mandatory. Airport‑to‑city fare ≈ ₹350‑₹500.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Base ₹25, then ₹12 per km; negotiate before boarding; typical city ride (e.g., CST → Bandra) ≈ ₹120.  
- **App‑based cabs (Uber/Ola):** Same base as taxis; surge pricing may apply during peak hours or festivals.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Maharashtrian street food with heavy use of coconut oil and peanuts.  
- Allergy risks: Groundnut‑based chutneys, coconut milk, and occasional seafood allergens.  
- Vegetarian options: **Very high**; most street stalls offer veg versions, and many restaurants are purely vegetarian.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: **Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital** (Andheri, ~10 km from South Mumbai) – 24 hr emergency, English‑speaking staff.  
- Recommended to carry: basic first‑aid kit, oral rehydration salts, any personal medication, and a copy of health insurance.

- Nearest state bus depot: **Mumbai Central Bus Station (ST Stand)**.  
- Inter‑city bus to Pune: **₹300 – ₹500**, frequency every 30 minutes.

## Hidden Gems

**Sewri Flamingo Sanctuary**  
- Why it’s special: Seasonal flamingo roosting (Nov‑Mar) on a mud‑flat, minimal tourist crowd.  
- Entry: Free; small donation for bird‑watching guide (optional ₹100).  
- How to reach: Take a local train to **Kurla** (≈ 5 km) then a rickshaw; or a direct auto from Bandra (≈ 15 km).  
- Best time: Early morning (05:30–08:00) during low tide.  
- Suitability: Flat boardwalk, **suitable for toddlers (in carrier) and elderly**, but beware of mosquitoes – carry repellent.

**Banganga Tank and Walk**  
- Why it’s special: 12th‑century step‑well surrounded by temples; a quiet spiritual walk away from the city buzz.  
- Entry: Free; small donation for maintenance (₹20).  
- How to reach: Walk from **Walkeshwar Temple** (South Mumbai) or take a local bus to **Malabar Hill**.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (16:00–18:00) when the sun casts gentle light on the water.  
- Suitability: Uneven stone steps (≈ 5 m rise) – **not ideal for toddlers in strollers**, but manageable for elderly with good footwear.

**Madhavendra Bhavan (Heritage House, Bandra)**  
- Why it’s special: Restored 19th‑century bungalow with a rooftop café offering panoramic city views, rarely listed in guidebooks.  
- Entry: ₹150/adult, ₹80/child (under 12).  
- How to reach: Auto‑rickshaw from Bandra Station (≈ 2 km).  
- Best time: Sunset (18:30–19:30) for cityscape photography.  
- Suitability: Rooftop has mild stairs; **suitable for elderly, but toddlers should be carried**.

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide strong 4G/5G coverage across most of Mumbai.  
- Patchy zones: Some dense slum alleys (e.g., Dharavi) and the interiors of large parks may have weak signals.

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs at **CST**, **Bandra**, **Andheri**, and **Juhu** (SBI, HDFC, ICICI).  
- Cash is still preferred at many street‑food stalls; card acceptance improves in malls and upscale restaurants.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at the airport (Jio, Airtel kiosks) or at authorized retail stores in Bandra and Andheri.  
- Jio offers the best overall coverage in Mumbai; ensure you bring passport, 2 × 2 cm photo, and a copy of your ID for activation.
