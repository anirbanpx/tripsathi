# Agra Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Delhi → Agra → Fatehpur Sikri → Sikandra**

The order follows a clockwise loop that minimises back‑tracking. Delhi‑Agra is on the high‑speed Yamuna Expressway (NH 44), allowing a fresh start after the long drive. Visiting the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort first lets travellers acclimatise to the hot sun before moving to the more shaded sites at Fatehpur Sikri and finally the quieter Akbar’s Tomb at Sikandra, reducing fatigue for toddlers and elderly.

Driving times:  
- Delhi → Agra: 2.5 hrs (Yamuna Expressway, NH 44)  
- Agra → Fatehpur Sikri: 1 hr (NH 93)  
- Fatehpur Sikri → Sikandra: 30 min (state road SH 76)

**Alternative routing:** Arrive by train at Agra Cantt, explore the city core, then take a local bus or hired car to Fatehpur Sikri and Sikandra, returning to Agra for departure.

## Agra City Core

**Key places:** Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Itmad‑ud‑Daulah, Mehtab Bagh, Aram Bagh  

### Taj Mahal  
- Entry: ₹1,100 adult (Indian), ₹1,300 foreigner; ₹0 under 3 years; ₹540 child (5‑15 yrs)  
- **Suitability:** 3 km of marble pathways, uneven steps; not recommended for toddlers without a carrier; elderly can use wheelchair rentals available at the gate.  
- **Best time:** Sunrise (5:30‑6:30 am) for cool temperatures and minimal crowds.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Fridays for prayer; open all other days.

### Agra Fort  
- Entry: ₹550 adult, ₹260 child (5‑15 yrs)  
- **Suitability:** Rough stone stairs and long corridors; toddlers may tire quickly; elderly should avoid the upper terraces unless assisted.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (8‑10 am) before the heat peaks.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None, but the fort’s interior can be sweltering in May‑June.

### Itmad‑ud‑Daulah (Baby Taj)  
- Entry: ₹300 adult, ₹150 child  
- **Suitability:** Small garden‑style complex, 1 km walk; toddlers can manage with a stroller on the paved path; elderly find it comfortable.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4‑5 pm) for soft lighting.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Mehtab Bagh (Moonlight Garden)  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child; free for children < 5 yrs.  
- **Suitability:** Flat lawns, wheelchair‑friendly; perfect for toddlers to run and for elderly to sit on benches.  
- **Best time:** Sunset (6‑7 pm) for unobstructed Taj view.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Fridays.

### Aram Bagh (Mughal Garden)  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; free for toddlers.  
- **Suitability:** Gentle pathways, shaded pavilions; fully suitable for toddlers and elderly.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (7‑9 am) when the garden is cool.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

**Trust issue for Agra City Core:**  
- *Taxi over‑charging*: Drivers often take the longer Yamuna Expressway loop or claim meter malfunction.  
- **Recommended approach:** Pre‑book a radio‑taxi (e.g., Uber/Ola) or negotiate a flat rate of ₹1,200 for a round‑trip from the Taj area before starting the journey.

**Agra City food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Petha* (sweet pumpkin candy), *Mughlai Biryani*, *Dal Moth*, *Bedmi Poori*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Idli* with plain coconut chutney, *Khichdi* (soft rice‑lentil porridge), *Plain boiled potatoes*, *Soft roti* with ghee.  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most street stalls and restaurants offer fully veg plates, though many Mughlai specialties contain meat.

## Fatehpur Sikri (UNESCO‑listed Mughal City)

**Key places:** Buland Darwaza, Panch Mahal, Jodha Bai Palace, Salim Chowk, Tomb of Salim  

### Buland Darwaza  
- Entry: ₹340 adult, ₹170 child (5‑15 yrs)  
- **Suitability:** 13 m high gateway with steep steps; not suitable for toddlers; elderly may find the climb strenuous.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (8‑9 am) to avoid glare on the red sandstone.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Panch Mahal  
- Entry: ₹340 adult, ₹170 child  
- **Suitability:** Elevated colonnade with narrow walkways; toddlers should be carried; elderly can enjoy the view but need support on the sloping stairs.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4‑5 pm) for golden‑hour photography.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Jodha Bai Palace (Jodha Bai’s House)  
- Entry: ₹340 adult, ₹170 child  
- **Suitability:** Flat courtyard and low arches; suitable for toddlers with supervision; elderly find it easy to navigate.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑day (11‑12 pm) when interior light is bright.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Salim Chowk (Market Square)  
- Entry: Free  
- **Suitability:** Open square with vendors; toddlers can be distracted by stalls; elderly should watch for uneven cobbles.  
- **Best time:** Evening (6‑8 pm) for local street food.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Tomb of Salim (Prince Jahangir)  
- Entry: ₹340 adult, ₹170 child  
- **Suitability:** Small, well‑maintained garden; fully suitable for toddlers and elderly.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (9‑10 am) for cooler temperatures.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

**Trust issue for Fatehpur Sikri:**  
- *“Donation” scam*: Vendors near the entrance ask for “donations” to preserve the site, inflating amounts.  
- **Recommended approach:** Decline unsolicited donations; official entry fees are printed at the gate. If you wish to support heritage, use the ASI’s online donation portal.

**Fatehpur Sikri food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Kachori* with *Aloo* stuffing, *Mughlai Korma*, *Gulab Jamun*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Plain rice with dal*, *Soft boiled carrots*, *Mild paneer tikka* (no spice).  
- Vegetarian availability: Very high; most local eateries are pure veg, though some Mughlai dishes contain meat.

## Sikandra & Surroundings

**Key places:** Akbar’s Tomb, Chini Ka Rauza, Mankameshwar Temple, Sikandra Garden  

### Akbar’s Tomb (Sikandra)  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹25 child (5‑15 yrs)  
- **Suitability:** 2 km of paved walk, gentle slopes; toddlers can use a stroller; elderly may need a short rest on benches.  
- **Best time:** Late morning (10‑11 am) when the marble glitters.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Chini Ka Rauza (Mughal Tomb)  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child; free for toddlers.  
- **Suitability:** Small structure with smooth stone steps; fully suitable for toddlers and elderly.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑day (12‑1 pm) for interior light.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Mankameshwar Temple (Ancient Shiva Temple)  
- Entry: Free (small donation optional)  
- **Suitability:** Steep stone steps (≈30 steps); not recommended for toddlers; elderly may need assistance.  
- **Best time:** Early evening (5‑6 pm) for cooler air and temple bells.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; closed on Mondays for maintenance.

### Sikandra Garden (private Mughal garden)  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; free for toddlers.  
- **Suitability:** Flat lawns, shaded benches; ideal for families with toddlers and for elderly relaxation.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4‑5 pm) for gentle breeze.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

**Trust issue for Sikandra:**  
- *Guide price inflation*: Unlicensed guides quote ₹2,500 per day for a “full‑day” tour, far above the market rate of ₹800‑₹1,000.  
- **Recommended approach:** Hire ASI‑approved guides at the ticket counter or use reputable online platforms (e.g., Viator) with transparent pricing.

**Sikandra food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Mughlai Gosht*, *Shahi Paneer*, *Lassi* (sweetened).  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Plain roti with ghee*, *Mild khichdi*, *Steamed carrots*.  
- Vegetarian availability: Strong; most restaurants serve a full veg menu alongside meat options.

## General Agra Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Cool (8‑25 °C), monuments fully open, sunrise/sunset photography optimal.  
- **April – June:** Hot (30‑45 °C); early‑morning visits only; indoor museums preferred.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon; occasional waterlogging at Mehtab Bagh, Taj Mahal foggy; indoor sites still accessible.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi (metered):** ₹15 per km base, plus ₹12 per km after the first 5 km; airport‑to‑city flat ₹500.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** ₹12 per km (metered) or ₹150 for a 10 km ride; negotiate before boarding.  
- **App‑based:** Uber/Ola – typical fare ₹300‑₹600 for city centre trips; surge pricing peaks during Taj Mahal sunrise hours.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Mughlai with liberal use of ghee, saffron, and nuts; coconut oil appears in sweets (e.g., *Petha*).  
- Allergy risks: Ground nuts and dairy are common; ask for “no‑nut” preparation if needed.  
- Vegetarian options: Abundant; most street stalls serve *dal*, *sabzi*, *roti*, and *puri* without meat.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: **Moolchand Hospital**, 5 km from the Taj Mahal, 24‑hour emergency, cardiology and orthopaedics.  
- Carry: Basic first‑aid kit, any prescription meds, a copy of health insurance, and a small pack of oral rehydration salts for heat‑related dehydration.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- Nearest station: **Agra Cantt** (≈2 km from city centre).  
- Key trains from Delhi: *Gatimaan Express* (train 12055, 2 hrs 15 min) and *Shatabdi Express* (train 12001, 2 hrs).  
- From Mumbai: *Kolkata‑Mumbai Express* (train 12951) stops at Agra Cantt; journey ≈ 24 hrs.

**By air:**  
- Nearest airport: **Kheria Airport (AGR)**, 7 km from the city centre.  
- Airlines: IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet.  
- Approx. one‑way fare from Delhi: ₹2,500 – ₹5,000; from Mumbai: ₹4,000 – ₹7,500.

**By road:**  
- Highway: **Yamuna Expressway (NH 44)** from Delhi (200 km).  
- Nearest state bus depot: **Agra ISBT (Inter‑State Bus Terminal)**, 3 km from Taj area; frequent UPSRTC and private buses to Delhi, Jaipur, and Lucknow.  
- Typical inter‑city bus fare: ₹300 – ₹600 (Delhi‑Agra).

## Hidden Gems

**Aram Bagh**  
- Why special: One of the oldest Mughal gardens, rarely crowded, offers a quiet riverside walk and a small museum of Mughal horticulture.  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; free for toddlers.  
- How to reach: Auto‑rickshaw from Agra Fort (≈ 3 km).  
- Best time: Early morning (7‑9 am) for cool breeze.  
- Suitability: Flat paths, benches; perfect for toddlers and elderly.

**Chini Ka Rauza**  
- Why special: A petite marble tomb with intricate Persian‑style tile work, often missed by mainstream tours.  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child; free for toddlers.  
- How to reach: Walkable from Akbar’s Tomb (≈ 500 m).  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12‑1 pm) for interior lighting.  
- Suitability: Smooth stone steps; stroller‑friendly; elderly can rest on nearby benches.

**Mankameshwar Temple**  
- Why special: Ancient Shiva shrine perched on a hill, offering panoramic views of the Yamuna and a spiritual contrast to the Mughal monuments.  
- Entry: Free (optional ₹20 donation).  
- How to reach: Auto‑rickshaw to the base, then a short uphill walk (≈ 30 m).  
- Best time: Evening (5‑6 pm) for cooler air and temple bells.  
- Suitability: Steep steps; not for toddlers; elderly may need assistance.

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide 4G coverage across the city centre and major tourist sites.  
- Patchy signal near the Yamuna riverbanks and at the outskirts of Fatehpur Sikri; consider offline maps.

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs: SBI (near Taj Mahal), HDFC (near Agra Fort), ICICI (near Kheria Airport).  
- Cash is preferred at small stalls; most restaurants accept cards, but a ₹500‑₹1,000 backup is advisable.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at the airport kiosk (Jio or Airtel) for immediate activation, or at any authorised retailer in Agra’s market area.  
- Jio offers the best overall coverage in Agra and surrounding heritage sites.
