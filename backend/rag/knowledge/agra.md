# Agra Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Delhi → Agra → Fatehpur Sikri**

Travelers start from Delhi because the international airport and major rail hub are there, and the Yamuna Expressway offers a smooth, low‑traffic corridor to Agra. Visiting Agra first lets you see the Taj Mahal and Agra Fort before heading west to Fatehpur Sikri, which is only 40 km away and best explored after the main Agra sights to avoid back‑tracking.

Driving times:  
- Delhi → Agra: 2.5 hours (Yamuna Expressway, NH‑44)  
- Agra → Fatehpur Sikri: 1 hour (State Highway 2, SH‑2)

**Alternative routing:** Delhi → Mathura → Agra → Fatehpur Sikri. This loop adds a short stop at the Krishna‑related sites in Mathura (≈30 km from Delhi) and is useful for pilgrim groups or families wanting a religious side‑trip before the main tourist circuit.

## Agra City

**Key places:** Taj Mahal, Agra Fort, Mehtab Bagh, Itmad‑ud‑Daulah (Baby Taj), Akbar’s Tomb, Sadar Bazaar

**Taj Mahal:**  
- Entry: ₹ 1,300 adult (Indian), ₹ 50 child (under 12, no separate ticket for toddlers), ₹ 250 for foreign tourists.  
- Suitability: 5 km paved pathway, wheelchair‑friendly ramps at the main entrance; however, the marble steps and uneven flooring inside the dome area make it **not suitable for toddlers under 3** and **mobility‑limited elderly travellers**.  
- Best time: Early morning (sunrise) to avoid crowds and heat; also the light is optimal for photography.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Fridays for prayer; open all other days year‑round.

**Agra Fort:**  
- Entry: ₹ 600 adult, ₹ 300 for Indian students, ₹ 50 child (under 12).  
- Suitability: Large courtyards and flat ramped walkways are **suitable for toddlers (stroller‑friendly) and elderly travellers**, but the inner citadel has steep staircases; a portable stair‑lift can be hired at the gate.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (3‑5 pm) when the fort’s red sandstone glows.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but the inner palace may be temporarily closed for conservation work.

**Mehtab Bagh:**  
- Entry: ₹ 50 adult, free for children under 12.  
- Suitability: Fully level garden with benches; **ideal for toddlers (wide lawns for play) and elderly visitors**.  
- Best time: Sunset, for a mirrored view of the Taj across the Yamuna.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Itmad‑ud‑Daulah (Baby Taj):**  
- Entry: ₹ 30 adult, free for children.  
- Suitability: Small marble structure with narrow corridors; **not recommended for toddlers** due to low ceilings and for elderly travellers with limited mobility.  
- Best time: Mid‑morning when light penetrates the marble inwards.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue – Ticket overcharging at Taj Mahal:**  
- Some unofficial vendors near the Taj claim to sell “fast‑track” tickets at inflated rates (₹ 2,500–₹ 3,000).  
- Recommended approach: Purchase tickets only from the official ticket counter or via the government‑run “e‑Taj” portal; keep the receipt for any refunds.

**Agra City food:**  
- Signature dishes: Petha (soft pumpkin candy), Mughlai biryani, Dal‑moth, Bedai (deep‑fried lentil dumplings), Kachori with aloo stuffing.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft idli, plain rice with dal, khichdi (rice‑lentil porridge), boiled egg yolk, and plain roti with ghee.  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most street stalls serve veg versions of kebabs (paneer tikka) and biryani, though some Mughlai specialties contain meat broth—ask “veg” explicitly.

## Fatehpur Sikri

**Key places:** Buland Darwaza, Jama Masjid, Panch Mahal, Diwan‑i‑Khas, Sheesh Mahal

**Buland Darwaza:**  
- Entry: ₹ 30 adult, free for children.  
- Suitability: Wide stair‑case (30 m) leading to a massive arch; **not suitable for toddlers** (steep steps) but **manageable for elderly travellers with a walking stick**.  
- Best time: Early morning to avoid the harsh sun on the sandstone.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Jama Masjid (Fatehpur Sikri):**  
- Entry: ₹ 20 adult, free for children.  
- Suitability: Flat courtyard and low‑rise prayer hall; **suitable for toddlers (open space) and elderly**.  
- Best time: Late afternoon when the interior is illuminated by the setting sun.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Panch Mahal:**  
- Entry: ₹ 20 adult, free for children.  
- Suitability: Five‑tiered pavilion with narrow stairways; **not recommended for toddlers** and **caution for elderly with joint issues**.  
- Best time: Mid‑day for clear views of the surrounding gardens.  
- Seasonal closure: Occasionally closed for restoration (check local notices).

**Trust issue – Fake “private guide” at Fatehpur Sikri:**  
- Unlicensed guides often approach tourists near the Buland Darwaza, offering “exclusive” tours at ₹ 1,500–₹ 2,000 per hour.  
- Recommended approach: Use the official guide desk inside the complex (₹ 300 for a half‑day guide) or hire a guide through the Uttar Pradesh Tourism website; decline unsolicited offers.

**Fatehpur Sikri food:**  
- Signature dishes: Bedmi poori with aloo sabzi, Mughlai kebab (non‑veg), Gajar ka halwa (carrot sweet), Petha (same as Agra).  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft roti with ghee, plain boiled rice with dal, mashed potatoes, and fresh curd.  
- Vegetarian availability: Strong; most local eateries serve veg versions of kebabs (paneer) and the staple roti‑dal combo.

## General Agra Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Cool (10‑25 °C), clear skies; all monuments fully open, sunrise/sunset photography optimal.  
- **April – June:** Warm (30‑40 °C); early morning visits recommended; water scarcity may affect street‑food stalls.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon; heavy rain can make the Taj’s marble slippery and some garden areas (Mehtab Bagh) waterlogged; indoor attractions like museums remain accessible.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at ₹ 15 for the first km, then ₹ 12 per km; airport‑to‑city trips average ₹ 1,200‑₹ 1,500. Negotiating is discouraged; insist on a meter or a pre‑agreed app fare.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Fixed‑rate for short hops (e.g., Agra Fort to Taj Mahal) is ₹ 150‑₹ 200; longer rides (city‑to‑Fatehpur Sikri) are ₹ 300‑₹ 350. Always confirm price before boarding.  
- **App‑based options:** Uber and Ola operate in Agra; typical city rides ₹ 200‑₹ 400. Auto‑rickshaws can be booked via “Rapido” for ₹ 150‑₹ 250.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine is North Indian with Mughlai influence—rich in ghee, cream, nuts, and aromatic spices. Coconut oil is rare; pork is virtually absent.  
- **Allergy risks:** Groundnuts and sesame are common in sweets; ask “no nuts” if allergic.  
- **Vegetarian options:** Abundant; most street stalls and restaurants offer veg thali, paneer dishes, and dal‑based meals. However, some “Mughlai” items contain hidden meat broth—verify “veg” when ordering.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest high‑quality hospital: **Mayo Hospital, Agra** (≈5 km from Taj Mahal, 24‑hour emergency, English‑speaking staff).  
- Recommended to carry a basic medical kit (band‑aids, antiseptic wipes, oral rehydration salts, any chronic medication) and a copy of health insurance. In case of severe injury, private ambulance services can be booked via “Arogya Cabs” at ₹ 2,500‑₹ 3,000 for city‑wide coverage.
