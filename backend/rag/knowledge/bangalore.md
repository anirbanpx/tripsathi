# Bangalore / Bengaluru Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Kempegowda International Airport → Central Bangalore → Nandi Hills**

The airport lies north of the city on NH 44; starting here lets you acclimatise to Bangalore’s traffic before heading south to the core attractions, then finish with the hill‑top view at Nandi Hills to avoid back‑tracking through congested downtown during the evening rush.

Driving times:  
- Kempegowda International Airport → Central Bangalore: ~30 km (NH 44) – 1 hour (peak traffic may add 30 min)  
- Central Bangalore → Nandi Hills: ~60 km (NH 75) – 1.5 hours (steady flow on the highway)

**Alternative routing:** Arrive via Bangalore‑Mysore Highway (NH 275) to the southern suburbs, explore Electronic City first, then cut across the Outer Ring Road to the airport. This loop reduces city‑center congestion for travellers arriving late afternoon.

## Central Bangalore

**Key places:** Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park, Bangalore Palace, Vidhana Soudha, Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace, ISKCON Temple

**Lalbagh Botanical Garden:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (under 12)  
- Suitability: 2 km of paved pathways, gentle slopes; stroller‑friendly but may be crowded on weekends – **suitable for toddlers and elderly with assistance**.  
- Best time of day: 6 am–8 am for cooler air and blooming flowers.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but the glasshouse is closed on Mondays.

**Cubbon Park:**  
- Entry: Free (₹0)  
- Suitability: Flat, well‑maintained lawns; **ideal for toddlers, seniors, and families with young kids**.  
- Best time: Early morning or late afternoon for shade under mature trees.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Bangalore Palace:**  
- Entry: ₹230 adult, ₹115 child (under 12)  
- Suitability: 1 km of uneven, stone‑paved walk and steep steps – **not recommended for toddlers or mobility‑limited seniors**.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4 pm–6 pm) when light filters through the arches.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays.

**Tipu Sultan’s Summer Palace:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child  
- Suitability: Flat marble floors, low steps – **suitable for toddlers (with supervision) and elderly**.  
- Best time: Mid‑morning to avoid glare on the frescoes.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**ISKCON Temple (Bangalore):**  
- Entry: Free (donations welcome)  
- Suitability: Wide corridors, wheelchair ramps – **fully accessible for toddlers, seniors, and wheelchair users**.  
- Best time: Evening aarti (7 pm–8 pm) for cultural immersion.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for Central Bangalore:**  
- Unofficial “guide” vendors near Vidhana Soudha often charge ₹500‑₹800 for a “quick city tour” that merely repeats information found on free plaques.  
- Recommended approach: Use the Bangalore Metro (Namma Metro) for self‑guided tours; download the official Namma Metro app for route maps and audio guides.

**Central Bangalore food:**  
- Signature dishes: Bisi Bele Bath, Masala Dosa, Mysore Pak, Ragi Mudde, Filter Coffee.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft idli, plain rice with dal, curd rice (thayir sadam), butter‑soft dosa without spicy chutney.  
- Vegetarian availability: **Very high** – most eateries serve pure‑vegetarian South Indian fare; non‑veg options are clearly labelled.

## North Bangalore (Whitefield & Nandi Hills)

**Key places:** Whitefield Shopping Complex, Nandi Hills, Bhoga Nandana (temple), Bangalore International Exhibition Centre

**Nandi Hills:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (under 12)  
- Suitability: 3 km steep climb with rocky patches; **not suitable for toddlers or seniors with limited mobility**.  
- Best time of day: Sunrise (5 am–7 am) for panoramic views and cooler temperatures.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed during heavy monsoon (June – September) due to landslide risk.  

**Whitefield Shopping Complex:**  
- Entry: Free (₹0) – shopping area.  
- Suitability: Flat, air‑conditioned corridors; **suitable for all ages**, but crowds can be dense on weekends.  
- Best time: Weekday mornings (10 am–12 pm) to avoid peak footfall.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for North Bangalore:**  
- Ticket touts at Nandi Hills gate often sell “premium” passes at ₹150 instead of the official ₹20, claiming they include “guided sunrise”.  
- Recommended approach: Purchase tickets only at the official counter or via the Karnataka Tourism app; carry exact change to avoid over‑charging.

**North Bangalore food:**  
- Signature dishes: Ragi Rotti, Akki Rotti, Chicken Biryani (non‑veg), Coconut Ladoo.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft ragi rotti with a thin layer of ghee, plain rice with a dollop of ghee, mild vegetable sambar.  
- Vegetarian availability: **Excellent** – most stalls are vegetarian; non‑veg items are clearly marked.

## South Bangalore (Electronic City & Bannerghatta)

**Key places:** Banner Bannerghatta National Park, Electronic City, Innovative Film City, HSR Layout

**Bannerghatta National Park (Safari & Zoo):**  
- Entry: ₹260 adult, ₹130 child (under 12) – includes safari ticket.  
- Suitability: Safari vehicle rides on bumpy 2 km tracks; **not suitable for toddlers under 3 or seniors with back problems**.  
- Best time of day: 9 am–11 am before midday heat.  
- Seasonal closure: Safari closed during heavy rain (July – August).  

**Electronic City (IT hub & shopping):**  
- Entry: Free (₹0) – business district.  
- Suitability: Flat, well‑maintained sidewalks; **suitable for all ages**, though traffic can be dense during rush hour.  
- Best time: Early morning (7 am–9 am) for quieter streets.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Innovative Film City:**  
- Entry: ₹250 adult, ₹150 child (under 12)  
- Suitability: 1.5 km of paved walk with occasional steps; **moderately suitable for toddlers (must be carried) and seniors (use benches)**.  
- Best time: Late morning (10 am–12 pm) when attractions are open but crowds are moderate.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for South Bangalore:**  
- “Fast‑track” safari tickets sold by unofficial vendors near Bannerghatta often cost double the official price and may be invalid.  
- Recommended approach: Book safari tickets directly on the Bannerghatta website or at the park’s official kiosk; verify the ticket’s hologram.

**South Bangalore food:**  
- Signature dishes: Ragi Mochi, Mysore Masala Dosa, Chicken Chettinad (non‑veg), Sweet Pongal.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft ragi muchi, plain rice with ghee, mild vegetable sambar, banana‑flavored curd.  
- Vegetarian availability: **High** – many restaurants specialise in vegetarian fare; non‑veg dishes are optional.

## General Bangalore Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – February:** Pleasant 15‑28 °C, all attractions open; festivals (Diwali, Karnataka Rajyotsava) increase crowds but offer cultural shows.  
- **March – May:** Hot 30‑38 °C; early morning visits to gardens and hills are advisable; water‑intensive attractions (aquariums) may have limited capacity.  
- **June – September:** Monsoon; average rainfall 900 mm, Nandi Hills and Bannerghatta Safari often closed; indoor museums (Visvesvaraya Industrial & Technological Museum) remain fully operational.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi / cab:** Metered fares start at ₹50; typical city‑wide rate ₹12‑₹15 per km. Airport‑to‑city: ₹900‑₹1,200 depending on traffic.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** ₹15‑₹20 per km; negotiate before boarding if meter is absent. Night surcharge: +₹30 per km after 10 pm.  
- **App‑based options:** Ola, Uber, and Rapido (bike‑share) operate city‑wide; surge pricing peaks during rush hour (7 am‑9 am, 6 pm‑8 pm).  

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: South Indian (rice, lentils, coconut oil, tamarind). Common allergens: coconut, peanuts, sesame.  
- Vegetarian options: **Abundant** – most restaurants serve a pure‑vegetarian menu; non‑veg items (chicken, mutton) are clearly labelled.  
- For sensitive travellers: request “no coconut” or “no peanuts” when ordering; most chefs accommodate.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospitals to central tourist zones: **Narayana Health (Bangalore) – Hosur Road**, **Manipal Hospital (Old Airport Road)**, **Apollo Hospital (Jayanagar)**. All have 24‑hour emergency departments and English‑speaking staff.  
- Recommended to carry: basic first‑aid kit, personal prescription meds, oral rehydration salts, and a small bottle of bottled water (tap water not safe for drinking).
