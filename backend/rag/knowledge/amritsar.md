# Amritsar Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Delhi → Amritsar → Wagah Border → Pul Kanjri**

Starting from Delhi on the historic Grand Trunk (GT) Road (NH 1) allows a smooth, mostly highway drive with regular fuel and food stops. Reaching Amritsar first lets you explore the dense historic core before heading west to the border (Wagah) and then south to the off‑beat Pul Kanjri, minimizing back‑tracking and avoiding night‑time highway travel on unfamiliar roads.

Driving times:  
- Delhi → Amritsar: 7 hours (NH 1/GT Road)  
- Amritsar → Wagah Border: 1 hour (NH 15)  
- Wagah Border → Pul Kanjri: 45 minutes (NH 15 → local road)

**Alternative routing:** Arrive via Amritsar airport and take a day‑trip loop Amritsar → Jallianwala Bagh → Gobindgarh Fort → Golden Temple, which is convenient for travelers flying in and preferring a compact city circuit.

## Central Amritsar (Golden Temple & Surroundings)

**Key places:** Golden Temple, Sri Guru Granth Sahib Museum, Akal Takht, Jogi Da Dera, Kesar Da Dhaba (food hub)

**Golden Temple (Harmandir Sahib):**  
- Entry: Free (₹0) for worshippers; ₹30 for museum ticket (optional)  
- **Suitability:** Fully accessible for toddlers (pram‑friendly ramps) and elderly (wheelchair‑accessible pathways). No steep steps.  
- **Best time of day:** Early morning (5:30–7:00 am) for serene ambience and cooler temperatures.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; open 24 hours year‑round.  

**Sri Guru Granth Sahib Museum (adjacent to Golden Temple):**  
- Entry: ₹30/adult, ₹15/child (3–12 yr) – free for children under 3.  
- **Suitability:** Quiet galleries, air‑conditioned; suitable for toddlers (short 30‑minute walk) and seniors.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑morning after temple visit to avoid crowd peak.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on major Sikh festivals (e.g., Guru Gurpurab) for maintenance.  

**Akal Takht (Sikh Parliament):**  
- Entry: Free (₹0) – guided tours ₹150/adult (pre‑booked).  
- **Suitability:** Small staircases (≈2 m) – not recommended for toddlers in strollers; seniors may need assistance.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4–5 pm) when the sanctum is quieter.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Sikh holy days.  

**Trust issue – “Temple Donation Scam”:**  
- Some vendors near the complex claim to collect “official” donations for temple upkeep and charge inflated rates for “blessing” packets.  
- Recommended approach: Donate only at the official donation box inside the temple courtyard; ignore unsolicited pitch‑men.

**Central Amritsar food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Amritsari Kulcha*, *Lassi* (sweet & salted), *Chole Bhature*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Idli* with mild coconut chutney, *Plain rice with dal* (soft lentils), *Masala Dosa* (mild spice).  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most stalls are pure‑vegetarian, though some street vendors serve meat kebabs – ask “vegetarian only” explicitly.

## Historical Core (Jallianwala Bagh, Gobindgarh Fort, Partition Museum)

**Key places:** Jallianwala Bagh, Gobindgarh Fort, Partition Museum, Durgiana Temple

**Jallianwala Bagh:**  
- Entry: Free (₹0).  
- **Suitability:** Flat lawns, wheelchair‑friendly pathways; however, the central monument has a few steps – not ideal for toddlers in strollers. Seniors can navigate with assistance.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (7–9 am) to avoid heat and crowds.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.  

**Gobindgarh Fort:**  
- Entry: ₹180/adult Indian, ₹250/foreign; child (3–12 yr) ₹90.  
- **Suitability:** 1 km of paved walk; uneven sections near the cannon displays (≈3 km) – not suitable for toddlers; seniors may find the steep ramp challenging.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4–5 pm) when live cultural shows begin.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on major Sikh festivals (e.g., Baisakhi).  

**Partition Museum (Shahid Gandhi Museum):**  
- Entry: ₹50/adult, ₹25/child (3–12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** Small indoor galleries, air‑conditioned; fully accessible for toddlers (short walks) and elderly (no steps).  
- **Best time:** Mid‑day (12–2 pm) to combine with nearby lunch.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on national holidays (e.g., Republic Day).  

**Durgiana Temple:**  
- Entry: Free (₹0).  
- **Suitability:** Marble steps (≈5 m) – not stroller‑friendly; seniors may need a handrail.  
- **Best time:** Evening (7–9 pm) for illuminated view.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None, but heavy worship on Navratri.  

**Trust issue – “Ticket Overcharging at Gobindgarh”:**  
- Some unofficial counters quote ₹500+ for entry, claiming “premium” access.  
- Recommended approach: Purchase tickets only at the official kiosk inside the fort entrance; keep the receipt for any dispute.

**Historical Core food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Amritsari Fish*, *Makke di Roti* with *Sarson da Saag*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Plain boiled potatoes*, *Soft wheat chapati* with a dab of ghee, *Yogurt‑sweetened rice*.  
- Vegetarian availability: Good; most eateries offer a vegetarian thali.  

## Western Outskirts (Wagah Border & Pul Kanjri)

**Key places:** Wagah Border ceremony, Pul Kanjri, Maharaja Ranjit Singh Museum, Sikh Railway Museum

**Wagah Border Ceremony:**  
- Entry: Free (₹0) – organized by Indian Border Security Force; optional “VIP” seats ₹500 (limited).  
- **Suitability:** Large open ground; toddlers can sit on parents’ laps; seniors should bring a folding chair as seating is on concrete. No steep terrain.  
- **Best time:** Sunset (≈5:30 pm in winter, 7:30 pm in summer) – the ceremony peaks then.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; operates daily except during extreme weather (rare).  

**Pul Kanjri (heritage haveli near Wagah):**  
- Entry: ₹30/adult, ₹15/child (3–12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** Well‑maintained garden paths, gentle slopes – suitable for toddlers and elderly. No steep steps.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (8–10 am) before the site gets crowded.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Sikh festivals (e.g., Guru Nanak Jayanti).  

**Maharaja Ranjit Singh Museum:**  
- Entry: ₹50/adult, ₹20/child (3–12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** Small indoor rooms, wheelchair‑friendly entrance; toddlers can enjoy the toy‑like armor displays. Seniors may need a seat for the 30‑minute walkthrough.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑afternoon (2–4 pm) after border ceremony.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.  

**Sikh Railway Museum (near Pul Kanjri):**  
- Entry: ₹20/adult, ₹10/child (3–12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** Flat exhibition hall; fully accessible for toddlers and seniors.  
- **Best time:** Late morning (10–11 am).  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on major railway holidays.  

**Trust issue – “Border Taxi Overcharging”:**  
- Drivers near Wagah often quote ₹800 for a 10 km ride to the city, inflating the fare.  
- Recommended approach: Use a pre‑booked app‑based cab (e.g., Uber/Ola) or negotiate a fixed ₹300 fare before boarding.

**Western Outskirts food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Butter Chicken* (non‑veg), *Paneer Tikka*, *Chole Puri*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Plain boiled rice*, *Mild dal* (toor dal), *Soft roti* with a thin spread of butter.  
- Vegetarian availability: Very high; most restaurants serve a full vegetarian menu.

## Southern Suburbs (Maharaja Ranjit Singh Museum, Hall Bazaar, Kesar Da Dhaba)

**Key places:** Maharaja Ranjit Singh Museum, Hall Bazaar, Kesar Da Dhaba (food hub), Sheesh Mahal (heritage house)

**Maharaja Ranjit Singh Museum (city‑center branch):**  
- Entry: ₹50/adult, ₹20/child (3–12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** Ground‑floor galleries, wheelchair ramp; toddlers can explore the toy‑like swords. Seniors may need a bench (provided).  
- **Best time:** Early afternoon (1–3 pm).  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.  

**Hall Bazaar (shopping street):**  
- Entry: Free (₹0).  
- **Suitability:** Narrow lanes with occasional steps; not stroller‑friendly; seniors should be prepared for uneven cobbles.  
- **Best time:** Evening (6–9 pm) for vibrant market lights.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None, but stalls close early during monsoon (July‑September).  

**Kesar Da Dhaba (renowned eatery, not a hotel):**  
- Entry: Free (₹0) – food purchase separate.  
- **Suitability:** Indoor seating with high chairs; toddlers can sit on parents’ laps; seniors find seating comfortable.  
- **Best time:** Lunch (12–2 pm) for freshest rotis.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on major Sikh holidays.  

**Sheesh Mahal (heritage haveli, occasional tours):**  
- Entry: ₹100/adult, ₹50/child (3–12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** Two‑story building with stairs; not suitable for toddlers in strollers; seniors may need assistance on the upper floor.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑morning (10–11 am).  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during monsoon for preservation.  

**Trust issue – “Hall Bazaar Fake Handicraft Scam”:**  
- Vendors may claim “hand‑crafted” items are authentic antiques and charge ₹2,000+; many are mass‑produced.  
- Recommended approach: Ask for a receipt, compare prices at multiple stalls, and avoid impulse buys.  

**Southern Suburbs food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Amritsari Puri*, *Aloo Puri*, *Mithai* (sweet laddus).  
- For toddlers and young kids: *Plain boiled potatoes*, *Soft wheat paratha* with a dab of ghee, *Yogurt‑sweetened kheer*.  
- Vegetarian availability: Predominantly vegetarian; meat dishes are limited to a few specialty stalls.

## General Amritsar Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Pleasant 10‑25 °C, most attractions fully open; festivals (Diwali, Baisakhi) add cultural vibrancy but increase crowds.  
- **April – June:** Warm 30‑40 °C; Golden Temple and museums remain open, but outdoor sites (Jallianwala Bagh) can be uncomfortable midday.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon; occasional flooding near the River Sutlej; some outdoor markets close early; museum interiors stay dry.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at ₹25 for the first km, then ₹12 per km; negotiate only for out‑station trips.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Fixed rates for short hops (e.g., Golden Temple ↔ Hall Bazaar ≈ ₹30).  
- **App‑based cabs:** Uber/Ola widely available; typical city ride ₹150‑₹300 depending on distance and time.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Punjabi – heavy use of ghee, mustard oil, and dairy.  
- Allergy risks: Coconut oil (common in sweets), dairy for lactose‑intolerant travellers.  
- Vegetarian options: Abundant; most street stalls serve pure‑vegetarian fare; confirm “vegetarian only” to avoid hidden meat broth.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: **Dayanand Medical College & Hospital**, 3 km from the Golden Temple; 24‑hour emergency, English‑speaking staff.  
- Carry: Basic first‑aid kit, any chronic medication, a copy of vaccination records, and a small amount of cash for pharmacy purchases.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- Nearest railway station: **Amritsar Junction (ASR)**, 2 km from city centre.  
- Key trains: *Punjab Mail* (12345, Delhi → Amritsar, departs Delhi at 06:00 am, arrival 13:00 pm), *Kalka – Amritsar Express* (12431, from Kalka).  

**By air:**  
- Nearest airport: **Sri Guru Ram Dass Jee International Airport (ATQ)**, 5 km from city centre.  
- Airlines: Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Vistara.  
- Approximate one‑way fare (Delhi ↔ Amritsar): ₹2,500 – ₹5,000 (economy).  

**By road:**  
- Highway: **NH 1 (Grand Trunk Road)** connects Delhi, Jalandhar, and Amritsar.  
- Nearest state bus depot: **Amritsar Bus Stand (ISBT)**, 3 km from centre; frequent Punjab State Transport Corporation (PSTC) buses from Delhi (₹500‑₹700).  

## Hidden Gems

**Pul Kanjri:**  
- Uncrowded heritage haveli with a serene garden and a small museum of Ranjit Singh era artefacts.  
- Entry: ₹30/adult, ₹15/child.  
- Reach via a 45‑minute drive from Amritsar on NH 15; best visited early morning (8–10 am).  
- **Suitability:** Flat lawns, wheelchair‑friendly; toddlers can play safely; seniors enjoy shaded benches.

**Mata Shri Rani Sati Temple (Katra Bagh):**  
- Lesser‑known Sikh shrine with beautiful frescoes and quiet prayer halls.  
- Entry: Free (₹0).  
- Reach by local auto from Hall Bazaar (≈ 10 min). Best time: Late afternoon (4–5 pm) for soft lighting.  
- **Suitability:** Small steps (≈1 m) – not ideal for strollers; seniors may need assistance.

**Sikh Railway Museum (near Pul Kanjri):**  
- Small but informative exhibit on the role of railways in the 1947 Partition.  
- Entry: ₹20/adult, ₹10/child.  
- Reach by a short auto ride from Pul Kanjri; best visited mid‑morning.  
- **Suitability:** Fully accessible; toddlers can enjoy the miniature train models; seniors appreciate the seated displays.

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide 4G coverage across the city; dead zones are limited to the interior of Gobindgarh Fort and some narrow lanes of Hall Bazaar. Carry a portable charger for the occasional dip.

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs at **State Bank of India (SBI) – Amritsar Junction**, **HDFC Bank – Hall Bazaar**, and **Punjab National Bank – near Golden Temple**.  
- Cash is preferred for street food and small shops; most restaurants accept cards, but tip in cash.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at the airport arrival hall (Jai Singh Airport kiosk) or at any major mobile shop in Hall Bazaar.  
- Jio offers the best coverage in Amritsar’s outskirts (Wagah, Pul Kanjri); Airtel is strong in the city centre. Bring passport, passport‑size photo, and a copy of your visa for verification.
