# Alleppey Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Kochi → Alappuzha (Alle​ppey) → Kumarakom**

Kochi (Cochin) lies on the western coast and is the major air‑rail hub. The NH 66 (Cochin–Alappuzha Highway) is a straight, well‑maintained coastal road that lets travellers reach Alappuzha in under two hours, avoiding the fatigue of winding inland routes. From Alappuzha, the NH 47A (Alappuzha–Kumarakom Road) follows the Vembanad backwaters, offering scenic views and minimal traffic, making it ideal for a relaxed finish before heading to the backwater resorts of Kumarakom.

Driving times:  
- Kochi → Alappuzha: **1.5 h** (NH 66, 82 km)  
- Alappuzha → Kumarakom: **0.8 h** (NH 47A, 45 km)

**Alternative routing:** Arrive via Trivandrum (Thiruvananthapuram) and take the MC Road (State Highway 1) northwards to Alappuzha. This circuit is useful for south‑bound travellers who wish to explore the southern beaches before looping back north.

## Alappuzha Town

**Key places:** Alappuzha Beach, Alappuzha Lighthouse, Ambalapuzha Sree Krishna Temple, Alappuzha Canal Museum, Punnapra Beach

### Alappuzha Beach  
- **Entry:** Free  
- **Suitability:** Soft, flat sand; **suitable for toddlers** (no steep drops) and elderly (benches and shaded walkways).  
- **Best time:** Early morning (06:00–08:00) for sunrise and cooler breeze.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; open year‑round.

### Alappuzha Lighthouse  
- **Entry:** ₹15 adult, ₹10 child (3–12 y)  
- **Suitability:** 3 km of stairs; **not suitable for toddlers** or mobility‑limited travellers.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (16:00–18:00) to catch sunset over the Arabian Sea.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during heavy monsoon (July – August) for safety.

### Ambalapuzha Sree Krishna Temple  
- **Entry:** Free (optional donation ₹10–₹50)  
- **Suitability:** Flat courtyard, **suitable for toddlers**; stroller‑friendly.  
- **Best time:** Morning pooja (07:30–09:00) when the temple is less crowded.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; open all days.

### Alappuzha Canal Museum  
- **Entry:** ₹10 adult, ₹5 child (under 12)  
- **Suitability:** Small indoor space, **suitable for toddlers** (interactive models).  
- **Best time:** Afternoon (14:00–16:00) to avoid beach crowds.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on major Hindu festivals (e.g., Onam) for maintenance.

### Punnapra Beach  
- **Entry:** Free  
- **Suitability:** Gentle slope, **suitable for toddlers**; nearby benches for elderly.  
- **Best time:** Sunset (18:30–19:30) for calm sea and fewer visitors.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

#### Trust issue in Alappuzha Town  
- **Overcharging by auto‑rickshaws:** Drivers often quote flat rates that are 2–3 × the metered fare, especially for tourists unfamiliar with local distances.  
- **Recommended approach:** Insist on the meter (₹ 15 per km) or negotiate a clear per‑km rate before boarding; use app‑based cabs (Ola/Uber) where the fare is displayed upfront.

#### Alappuzha Town food  
- **Signature dishes:** Karimeen Pollichathu (pearl spot fish), Prawn Moilee, Avial, Appam with Vegetable Stew.  
- **For toddlers and young kids:** Soft Idli, plain rice with Dal, mashed banana with a drizzle of ghee, thin Dosa without spicy chutney.  
- **Vegetarian availability:** Excellent; most restaurants serve a full vegetarian menu, though fish dishes dominate the “specialties” section.

## Kuttanad (Backwaters)

**Key places:** Punnamada Lake, Houseboat Cruises, Nehru Trophy Boat Race, Pathiramanal Island, Muttar Boat House

### Punnamada Lake – Houseboat Cruise (Standard 2‑Berth)  
- **Entry:** ₹3,500 per night (incl. meals for 2 adults); extra ₹1,200 per child (under 12).  
- **Suitability:** Compact cabins with narrow passageways; **not ideal for toddlers** under 3 y (risk of slipping). Larger 3‑berth boats are recommended for families.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (06:00–09:00) when water is mirror‑still.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Monsoon (July – August) can cause rough waters; many operators suspend service.

### Nehru Trophy Boat Race (Vallam Kali) – Punnamada Lake  
- **Entry:** ₹100 adult, ₹50 child (5–12 y) – children under 5 are free but not admitted to the front rows.  
- **Suitability:** Massive crowds and loud horns; **not suitable for toddlers** or elderly with hearing issues.  
- **Best time:** Evening of the 5th Saturday of August (race starts at 17:30).  
- **Seasonal closure:** Event‑only; no entry outside race day.

### Pathiramanal Island (Bird Sanctuary)  
- **Entry:** ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (3–12 y) – children under 3 free.  
- **Suitability:** Short 15‑minute boat ride; **suitable for toddlers** (seated on parents’ laps). Walking trails are flat but may be muddy during monsoon.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (07:00–09:00) for birdwatching and cooler weather.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during heavy rains (July – September) to protect nesting birds.

### Muttar Boat House (Kayaking & Small Motorboat)  
- **Entry:** ₹200 per person for a 30‑minute ride.  
- **Suitability:** Stable motorboat; **suitable for toddlers** (seat belts provided).  
- **Best time:** Mid‑morning (10:00–12:00) when wind is gentle.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; operates year‑round.

### Krishnapuram Palace (Kayamkulam, 30 km from Alappuzha) – optional day‑trip  
- **Entry:** ₹30 adult, ₹20 child (5–12 y).  
- **Suitability:** Moderate walking on tiled floors; **not suitable for toddlers** (stairs at entrance).  
- **Best time:** Morning (09:00–11:00) before the midday heat.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on major festivals (Onam, Vishu).

#### Trust issue in Kuttanad  
- **Houseboat price inflation & hidden fuel surcharge:** Operators quote a low base price but add “fuel surcharge” of ₹500–₹800 per day after boarding.  
- **Recommended approach:** Get a written quote that itemises fuel, meals, and taxes; compare at least three operators before booking; use reputable agencies listed on the Kerala Tourism website.

#### Kuttanad food  
- **Signature dishes:** Karimeen Meen Curry, Banana Leaf Rice, Thoran (stir‑fried vegetables), Coconut Milk Pudding (Ada).  
- **For toddlers and young kids:** Soft boiled rice, plain mashed potatoes, banana slices, mild lentil soup (Kadalai Paruppu).  
- **Vegetarian availability:** Very high; most backwater eateries serve vegetarian thali with rice, sambar, and vegetable curries.

## Mararikulam (Coastal Village)

**Key places:** Marari Beach, St. Mary’s Church (Mararikulam), Coconut Grove, Beachside Snack Stalls

### Marari Beach  
- **Entry:** Free  
- **Suitability:** Wide, gently sloping sand; **suitable for toddlers** and elderly (shaded benches).  
- **Best time:** Sunset (18:00–19:30) for tranquil ambience.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### St. Mary’s Church (Mararikulam)  
- **Entry:** Free (donation ₹5–₹20 optional)  
- **Suitability:** Small steps at entrance; **suitable for toddlers** (hand‑rail available).  
- **Best time:** Early morning (07:00–09:00) when the interior is quiet.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Christmas and Easter for services.

### Coconut Grove (Local Agro‑tour)  
- **Entry:** ₹50 adult, ₹30 child (5–12 y) – children under 5 free.  
- **Suitability:** Flat walk among coconut palms; **suitable for toddlers** (no steep paths).  
- **Best time:** Mid‑afternoon (13:00–15:00) for shade.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

#### Trust issue in Mararikulam  
- **Beachside snack overcharging:** Vendors often charge ₹150 for a simple banana fritter that should cost ₹30–₹50.  
- **Recommended approach:** Buy snacks from certified “Food Safety” stalls (signage with FSSAI number) or carry packed meals; negotiate price before ordering.

#### Mararikulam food  
- **Signature dishes:** Fish Fry (Karimeen), Coconut Milk Curry, Kerala Parotta with Beef Curry (non‑veg), Tapioca with Fish Curry.  
- **For toddlers and young kids:** Soft Idli, plain boiled rice with a spoonful of ghee, mashed sweet potato, plain curd (Curd Rice).  
- **Vegetarian availability:** Good; most stalls serve vegetarian parotta with vegetable stew.

## General Alleppey Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – February:** Cool, dry weather (22‑30 °C); all attractions open; houseboat rides are calm.  
- **March – May:** Warm (30‑35 °C); beach activities pleasant; early morning boat rides recommended to avoid heat.  
- **June – September:** Monsoon (heavy rain, 200‑300 mm/month); backwater boat rides can be rough; some boat operators suspend service; festivals like Onam (August) bring crowds but also cultural shows.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered rate ₹15 per km; typical 10 km airport‑to‑town fare ₹150‑₹180. Negotiate before long‑distance trips; app‑based cabs (Ola, Uber) show ₹ ≈ ₹12‑₹14 per km.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Fixed rate ₹10 per km inside town; for airport trips many quote ₹120‑₹150 – verify against meter.  
- **App‑based options:** Ola and Uber operate from Cochin Airport to Alappuzha; bike‑share (Rapido) available for short intra‑town hops.

**Dietary notes:**  
- **Dominant cuisine:** Kerala coastal – heavy use of coconut oil, mustard seeds, and seafood.  
- **Allergy risks:** Coconut oil (common in all dishes), tamarind (sour), and occasional use of peanuts in chutneys.  
- **Vegetarian options:** Abundant; most restaurants serve a vegetarian thali (rice, sambar, avial, papadam). Pure‑veg travelers can request “no fish” – most kitchens comply.

**Medical:**  
- **Nearest quality hospital:** Government Medical College Hospital, Alappuzha (3 km from town centre, 24‑hour emergency, INR 500‑₹ 1,000 consultation).  
- **What to carry:** Basic first‑aid kit, oral rehydration salts, any chronic medication, and a copy of medical records. In case of severe injury, the nearest tertiary centre is in Kochi (≈ 80 km).

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- **Alappuzha Railway Station (ALLP):** 1 km from town centre.  
- **Key trains:**  
  - **12461/12462 Kanyakumari‑Delhi Express** (stops at Alappuzha).  
  - **16603/16604 Ernakulam‑Ahmedabad Express** (daily).  
- From Delhi, the journey takes ~ 36 h; from Mumbai, ~ 24 h.

**By air:**  
- **Cochin International Airport (COK):** 82 km, 2‑2.5 h drive via NH 66.  
- **Airlines:** IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, GoAir.  
- **Fare range:** ₹3,500 – ₹7,500 one‑way (economy) from major metros (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore).  

**By road:**  
- **Key highway:** NH 66 (Cochin–Alappuzha) and NH 47A (Alappuzha–Kumarakom).  
- **Nearest state bus depot:** Alappuzha KSRTC Bus Stand (central).  
- **Inter‑city bus frequency:** Every 30 min to Kochi, Trivandrum, and Kottayam.  
- **Typical fare:** ₹150 – ₹250 (Kochi‑Alappuzha), ₹500 – ₹600 (Trivandrum‑Alappuzha).

## Hidden Gems

**Pulinkunnu Church (Kuttanad)**  
- **Why special:** Quiet 19th‑century Protestant church set amid paddy fields; rarely visited by tourists, offering peaceful sunrise prayers.  
- **Entry/cost:** Free; small donation ₹5‑₹10 optional.  
- **How to reach:** Hire a local auto from Kuttanad bus stand (≈ ₹150 round‑trip).  
- **Best time:** Dawn (05:30–07:00) for soft light over the fields.  
- **Suitability:** Flat grounds, **suitable for toddlers** (no steps) and elderly (bench seating).

**Pathiramanal Island Bird Sanctuary** (already listed under Kuttanad but highlighted as hidden)  
- **Why special:** Home to over 90 migratory bird species; boat ride is short (≈ 15 min) and the island is largely un‑crowded outside the bird‑watching season.  
- **Entry/cost:** ₹20 adult, ₹10 child; boat ride included.  
- **How to reach:** Board from Alappuzha jetty (₹150 per boat).  
- **Best time:** Early morning (07:00–09:00) during October‑March.  
- **Suitability:** Flat walking trails, **suitable for toddlers** (short stroll) and elderly (rest benches).

**Kavalam Village (Backwater Homestay Experience)**  
- **Why special:** Authentic Kerala village life; stay in a traditional ‘nalukettu’ house with a private canal, far from the crowded houseboat circuits.  
- **Entry/cost:** Homestay ₹2,200 per night for a family of four (includes breakfast).  
- **How to reach:** Taxi from Alappuzha (₹300).  
- **Best time:** Post‑monsoon (October‑December) when fields are green.  
- **Suitability:** Gentle paddling; **suitable for toddlers** (shallow water) and elderly (no stairs inside the house).

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:** Jio and Airtel provide 4G coverage across Alappuzha town and the main backwater routes; Vi has spotty signal on isolated islands (e.g., Pathiramanal). Carry a portable Wi‑Fi hotspot if constant connectivity is required.

**ATMs:** Reliable SBI and HDFC ATMs are located at Alappuzha Railway Station and near the Alappuzha Beach parking area. Smaller private ATMs (Canara, Axis) operate in the market district but may have lower cash limits. Cash is preferred for beachside vendors; most restaurants accept cards.

**SIM cards:** Purchase at Cochin Airport (Jio or Airtel kiosks) or at the Alappuzha KSRTC bus depot. Jio offers the best coverage in the backwater region; Airtel is marginally better on the coastal stretch towards Mararikulam. Bring passport copy and a passport‑size photo for verification.
