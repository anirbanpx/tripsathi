# Alleppey / Alappuzha Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Kochi → Alappuzha → Kollam**

Kochi hosts the nearest international airport and railway hub; entering the backwater circuit from the north lets you travel downstream on the Vembanad‑Lake system, reducing fatigue and avoiding back‑tracking. Continuing south to Kollam keeps you on the same coastal highway, making onward travel to Trivandrum or the southern Kerala beaches seamless.

Driving times:  
- Kochi → Alappuzha: 1.5 hours (NH 66, also known as the Kochi‑Alappuzha coastal road)  
- Alappuzha → Kollam: 2 hours (NH 66, same coastal stretch)

**Alternative routing:** Arrive via Trivandrum (NH 66 north‑bound) and finish the loop at Kochi, or cut inland through MC Road (State Highway 1) to explore the interior tea‑plantation towns before re‑joining the coast at Alappuzha.

## Alappuzha Town (Backwater Hub)

**Key places:** Alappuzha Beach, Vembanad Lake Houseboat Jetty, Krishnapuram Palace, Alappuzha Lighthouse

**Alappuzha Beach:**  
- Entry: Free (₹20 for paid parking)  
- Suitability: 200 m of flat sand; wheelchair‑accessible ramps; toddlers can play safely under supervision, but avoid early morning tide rushes.  
- Best time of day: Early morning (sunrise) for cooler temperatures and fewer crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon (June‑September) can make the sea rough; swimming discouraged.

**Vembanad Lake Houseboat Cruise (Standard 2‑night):**  
- Entry: ₹6,500 adult per night (includes meals, fuel, crew); ₹5,000 child (under 12).  
- Suitability: Stable wooden hull; cabins with attached bathroom; toddlers can nap in the berth, but narrow corridors may challenge wheelchairs. Elderly travellers should avoid night‑time navigation during heavy rain.  
- Best time of day: Sunset on Day 1 for backwater photography; sunrise on Day 2 for bird‑watching.  
- Seasonal closure: Core routes remain open year‑round, but monsoon reduces visibility and may cancel night‑time excursions.

**Krishnapuram Palace:**  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹10 child (under 12).  
- Suitability: 1 km of paved pathways; uneven stone steps at the main gate (not wheelchair‑friendly). Toddlers can explore the garden under watch.  
- Best time of day: Mid‑afternoon when the palace interiors are lit for guided tours.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on major Hindu festivals (e.g., Vishu) for private events.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for this area:**  
- **Hidden fuel surcharge on houseboats:** Operators often quote a base price and later add “fuel surcharge” that can increase the bill by 30 %.  
- Recommended approach: Insist on a full‑breakdown quote before booking; verify the total includes fuel, meals, and any taxes. Use reputable aggregators or ask the local tourism office for vetted operators.

**Alappuzha food:**  
- Signature dishes: Karimeen Fry (Pearl Spot), Prawn Moilee, Kappa (tapioca) with Fish Curry, Erissery (pumpkin‑coconut mash).  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Idli, plain boiled rice with Dal, mashed banana, and steamed carrot sticks (all available at most family‑friendly eateries).  
- Vegetarian availability: Strong; most restaurants serve vegetarian thali, but many menu items are seafood‑centric; ask for “vegetarian only” to avoid hidden fish broth.

## Kumarakom (Bird Sanctuary & Lakeside)

**Key places:** Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary, Kumarakom Backwater Resort Jetty, Tharavadu Houseboat Dock

**Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary:**  
- Entry: ₹90 adult, ₹30 child (under 12).  
- Suitability: 2 km of boardwalks; wooden planks are level but can be slippery after rain – not ideal for toddlers who cannot walk unaided; elderly with limited mobility should use the nearby wheelchair‑friendly observation deck (₹20 extra).  
- Best time of day: Early morning (6 am‑9 am) when migratory birds are most active.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed during heavy monsoon (July‑August) due to water‑logging and safety concerns.

**Kumarakom Backwater Resort Jetty (Houseboat boarding):**  
- Entry: ₹5,500 adult per night (standard 2‑person cabin); ₹4,500 child (under 12).  
- Suitability: Same hull as Alappuzha cruises; cabins are compact but have grab rails for elderly. No stairs on the main deck – toddlers can sit on parents’ laps safely.  
- Best time of day: Late afternoon (4 pm‑6 pm) for a calm river breeze before sunset.  
- Seasonal closure: Limited service in September‑October when water levels drop; advance booking required.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for this area:**  
- **“All‑inclusive” houseboat packages that exclude tip and laundry:** Some operators quote a low price but later add mandatory “service charge” and “laundry fee.”  
- Recommended approach: Request a written itemised invoice; confirm that tip, laundry, and any extra meals are already included. Compare at least three quotes before confirming.

**Kumarakom food:**  
- Signature dishes: Meen Curry (Kerala fish), Avial (mixed vegetables in coconut), Puttu with Kadala (black gram) curry.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft Steamed Idli, plain rice with Dal, boiled sweet potato cubes, and fresh coconut water (served in small cups).  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most stalls serve pure vegetarian fare, though many highlight seafood specials – specify “vegetarian only” to avoid fish stock.

## General Alleppey / Alappuzha Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – February:** Pleasant 22‑30 °C weather; backwater cruises run daily; festivals like Onam (August‑September) add cultural colour but increase crowds.  
- **March – May:** Warm 30‑35 °C; early morning cruises still comfortable; beach activities peak, but sunscreen and hydration are essential.  
- **June – September:** Monsoon rains (≥200 mm/month); Vembanad water levels rise, making some houseboat routes unsafe; bird sanctuary often closed; travel slows, but rates drop sharply.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi / Cab:** From Kochi Airport to Alappuzha town: ₹1,200 – ₹1,500 (metered or fixed‑rate via app). Negotiating a flat rate is common; confirm total before boarding.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** ₹15 per km (standard meter); short hops (e.g., Alappuzha Beach to market) cost ₹60‑₹80. Drivers may quote “full‑day hire” – ask for a meter reading.  
- **App‑based options:** Uber and Ola operate in Alappuzha; typical fare for 5 km is ₹250‑₹300. Use the app to lock in price and share ride details with a companion.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Kerala coastal fare, heavy use of coconut oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves, and tamarind.  
- Allergy risks: Coconut (oil, milk), mustard seeds, and occasional groundnut oil in street snacks.  
- Vegetarian options: Widely available; most restaurants serve a vegetarian thali (rice, sambar, avial, papadam). However, many “vegetarian” dishes may be cooked with ghee or coconut oil – travellers with dairy intolerance should ask for “coconut‑free” preparation.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: Mahatma Gandhi Medical College Hospital, Alappuzha (≈5 km from the central market). 24‑hour emergency, well‑equipped ICU, and English‑speaking staff.  
- What to carry: Personal prescription meds, basic first‑aid kit (band‑aids, antiseptic, oral rehydration salts), insect repellent (DEET‑based), and a copy of any chronic condition documentation. In case of water‑borne illness, reputable pharmacies in Alappuzha town stock oral antibiotics and antidiarrheals.
