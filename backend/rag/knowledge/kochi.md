# Kochi Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Cochin International Airport → Fort Kochi → Ernakulam → Cherai**

This sequence follows the natural flow of traffic from the airport (south‑west entry) to the historic peninsula, then to the commercial hub, and finally to the coastal beach. It minimizes back‑tracking, avoids congested city centre during peak hours, and allows travelers to rest after the longest drive before reaching the beach.

Driving times:
- Cochin International Airport → Fort Kochi: 1.5 hours (NH 544 → NH 66, via Edapally)
- Fort Kochi → Ernakulam: 0.5 hours (Marine Drive Road, city traffic)
- Ernakulam → Cherai: 1 hour (NH 66, via Aluva)

**Alternative routing:** Enter via Aluva (NH 544) and head directly to Cherai, then loop back to Fort Kochi; this avoids the airport‑to‑city rush hour but adds a longer coastal stretch.

## Fort Kochi

**Key places:** Chinese Fishing Nets, St. Francis Church, Mattancherry Palace, Jew Town, Paradesi Synagogue, Fort Kochi Beach

**Chinese Fishing Nets:**
- Entry: Free
- Suitability: 200‑meter nets on uneven beach; 3 km walk required, not suitable for toddlers or mobility‑limited travellers.
- Best time: Early morning (sunrise) for cooler temperatures and photography.
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon (June‑Sept) makes the area slippery.

**St. Francis Church:**
- Entry: Free (optional donation ₹20)
- Suitability: Flat interior, wheelchair‑friendly ramp; suitable for toddlers and elderly.
- Best time: Late afternoon to avoid peak tourist crowds.
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Mattancherry Palace (Dutch Palace):**
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (3‑12 yrs), free for <3.
- Suitability: Paved paths, moderate steps; toddlers can use a stroller but watch steep sections; elderly may need assistance.
- Best time: Early morning to escape heat.
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Paradesi Synagogue:**
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (3‑12 yrs), free for <3.
- Suitability: Narrow doorway and uneven tiles; not ideal for strollers; elderly should be accompanied.
- Best time: Mid‑day for optimal interior lighting.
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Saturdays (Shabbat).

**Trust issue for Fort Kochi:**
- Overpriced “boat ride to Vypeen Island” sold by unofficial operators, quoting ₹500 per person and often skipping safety checks.
- Recommended approach: Use registered harbour services (fare ₹150‑₹200) and verify license plates before boarding.

**Fort Kochi food:**
- Signature dishes: Appam with vegetable stew, Kerala fish curry (Meen Curry), Beef Fry (non‑veg), Banana chips.
- For toddlers and young kids: Idli, plain rice with dal, mashed banana, soft appam with mild vegetable stew.
- Vegetarian availability: High; most eateries serve vegetarian meals, though coconut oil and mustard seeds are common. Request “no onion‑garlic” if needed.

## Ernakulam

**Key places:** Marine Drive, Kerala Folklore Museum, Ernakulam Shiva Temple, Lulu Mall, Hill Palace Museum

**Marine Drive:**
- Entry: Free
- Suitability: Flat promenade, wheelchair‑friendly; ideal for toddlers on stroller and elderly walkers.
- Best time: Sunset (6‑7 pm) for cool breeze and city lights.
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Kerala Folklore Museum:**
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (4‑12 yrs), free for <4.
- Suitability: Two‑floor building with elevator; suitable for toddlers and elderly.
- Best time: Weekday mornings to avoid school groups.
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays.

**Hill Palace Museum:**
- Entry: ₹25 adult, ₹10 child (3‑12 yrs), free for <3.
- Suitability: Large grounds with paved paths; 3 km walking required, not ideal for toddlers without carrier; elderly can rest on benches.
- Best time: Early hours to beat heat.
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Trust issue for Ernakulam:**
- “Lulu Mall discount cards” sold by street vendors promising 30 % off but are counterfeit; they may charge extra later.
- Recommended approach: Purchase official loyalty cards at the mall information desk; verify hologram and serial number.

**Ernakulam food:**
- Signature dishes: Kerala Sadya (banana‑leaf feast), Puttu with Kadala Curry, Kerala Parotta with Chicken Curry.
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft boiled egg, plain porridge (kanji), steamed rice with ghee and dal.
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most dishes are vegetarian, though coconut and peanuts are frequent—inform staff of allergies.

## Cherai

**Key places:** Cherai Beach, Pallipuram Fort, Dolphin Watch (seasonal)

**Cherai Beach:**
- Entry: Free
- Suitability: 3 km stretch of golden sand, gentle slope; safe for toddlers with supervision, elderly can walk but watch for sharp shells.
- Best time: Early morning (5‑7 am) for calm sea.
- Seasonal closure: None, but monsoon (June‑Sept) brings higher currents.

**Pallipuram Fort:**
- Entry: ₹10 adult, ₹5 child (3‑12 yrs), free for <3.
- Suitability: Small stone fort with uneven steps; not wheelchair accessible; toddlers should be carried.
- Best time: Late afternoon for soft light.
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Fridays.

**Trust issue for Cherai:**
- “Beachside camel rides” advertised online; operators often charge ₹800 per ride and lack proper permits.
- Recommended approach: Use government‑licensed boat operators for dolphin watching; avoid camel rides or negotiate a clear price before boarding.

**Cherai food:**
- Signature dishes: Fresh grilled fish (Karimeen), Coconut rice, Banana fritters.
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft appam with mild lentil stew, boiled potatoes, banana slices.
- Vegetarian availability: Good; many stalls serve veg items like vegetable fried rice, though fish dominates the menu.

## General Kochi Notes

**Best time to visit:**
- **October – February:** Pleasant 22‑30 °C, low humidity, major festivals (Onam) – all attractions open.
- **March – May:** Warm 30‑35 °C, sea breezes; beach activities fine, indoor museums can be hot.
- **June – September:** Southwest monsoon, heavy rain, sea rough; some beach activities limited, but festivals like Vishu; expect road delays.

**Transport pricing:**
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares from airport to city ₹800‑₹1,200; city‑to‑city rides ₹12 per km after first 2 km. Negotiate only if meter unavailable.
- **Auto/rickshaw:** ₹10‑₹12 per km; always ask for “meter on” or agree on ₹150 for a 10 km ride.
- **App‑based options:** Uber and Ola operate citywide; surge pricing typical evenings; base fare ₹80 plus ₹10 per km.

**Dietary notes:**
- Dominant cuisine: Kerala coastal cuisine, heavy use of coconut oil, mustard seeds, curry leaves, and seafood. Pork and beef appear in many non‑veg dishes.
- Allergy risks: Coconut, peanuts, and tamarind are common in gravies; inform staff if allergic.
- Vegetarian options: Widely available; most restaurants serve veg thali, appam, idli, and vegetable curries. Vegan‑friendly dishes are limited but can be requested.

**Medical:**
- Nearest quality hospital: Aster Medcity, Kalamassery (≈ 15 km from Fort Kochi) – 24‑hour emergency, multi‑specialty care.
- Recommended pack: Basic first‑aid kit, oral rehydration salts, any prescribed chronic meds, insect repellent, and a copy of medical records for senior travellers.
