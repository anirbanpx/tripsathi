# Coorg Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Bangalore → Madikeri → Bylakuppe → Mysore**

The circuit starts with Bangalore because the city hosts the nearest international airport and major rail hub. The drive to Madikeri (the district headquarters) follows the well‑maintained NH 275, allowing you to enjoy the gradual rise into the Western Ghats without excessive fatigue. Continuing to Bylakuppe keeps you on the same SH 88, a short detour that lets you experience the Tibetan settlement before descending back to the plains via Mysore, where a wider range of medical and transport facilities are available.

Driving times:  
- Bangalore → Madikeri: **5.5 hours** (NH 275 Bangalore‑Mysore → SH 88 Mysore‑Madikeri)  
- Madikeri → Bylakuppe: **1.5 hours** (SH 88 Madikeri‑Bylakuppe)  
- Bylakuppe → Mysore: **2 hours** (NH 275 Bylakuppe‑Mysore)

**Alternative routing:**  
Bangalore → Mysore → Bylakuppe → Madikeri → Hunsur → Bangalore makes a loop that avoids the single‑lane stretch between Madikeri and Bylakuppe, useful if you prefer a more relaxed return to the highway.

## Madikeri (Central Coorg)

**Key places:** Abbey Falls, Raja’s Seat, Madikeri Fort, Talacauvery (source of the Kaveri), Tadiandamol Peak, Dubare Elephant Camp, Nagarhole National Park  

### Abbey Falls  
- Entry: **₹15 adult**, **₹10 child (3‑12 yr)**; toddlers under 3 free but not permitted on wet paths.  
- **Suitability:** 300 m of uneven, slippery steps; **not suitable for toddlers** or mobility‑limited travellers.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (7:30‑9:00 am) to avoid mist and crowds.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during heavy monsoon (July‑September) for safety.

### Raja’s Seat  
- Entry: **₹20 adult**, **₹10 child**; free for children under 3.  
- **Suitability:** Gentle 200 m walk on paved terrace; **suitable for toddlers and elderly** with a bench at every viewpoint.  
- **Best time:** Sunset (5:30‑6:30 pm) for panoramic orange‑hued hills.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; open year‑round.

### Madikeri Fort  
- Entry: **₹10 adult**, **₹5 child**; toddlers free.  
- **Suitability:** Flat courtyard, a few steps; **suitable for toddlers and elderly**.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑day (11:00‑1:00 pm) when the museum’s audio guide is active.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on government holidays.

### Talacauvery (Source of the Kaveri)  
- Entry: **₹30 adult**, **₹15 child**; toddlers free but not allowed on steep descent.  
- **Suitability:** 1 km trek with stone steps; **not suitable for toddlers**; elderly may need assistance.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (6:00‑8:00 am) for clear skies and cooler temperature.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during monsoon‑induced landslides (July‑September).

### Tadiandamol Peak (Highest point in Coorg)  
- Entry: **₹250 per person** (park permit).  
- **Suitability:** 8 km trek with steep gradients; **not suitable for toddlers, children < 8 yr, or elderly with joint issues**.  
- **Best time:** Pre‑dawn (4:30‑6:00 am) to catch sunrise; avoid mid‑day heat.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed in heavy rain (June‑August).

### Dubare Elephant Camp (near Kushalnagar, 30 km from Madikeri)  
- Entry: **₹200 adult**, **₹150 child (5‑12 yr)**; toddlers under 5 not allowed on elephant rides.  
- **Suitability:** Elephant bathing area is flat; **suitable for toddlers to watch, but rides not for them**.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00‑5:30 pm) when elephants are most active.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during monsoon (July‑September).

### Nagarhole National Park (part of the larger reserve)  
- Entry: **₹300 adult**, **₹150 child (5‑12 yr)**; toddlers free but not allowed on safari vehicles.  
- **Suitability:** Safari vehicle is air‑conditioned with low steps; **suitable for elderly** but not for toddlers who cannot sit safely.  
- **Best time:** Early morning safari (6:00‑9:00 am) for wildlife sightings.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed for safari during heavy rains (July‑September).

#### Trust issue in Madikeri  
- **Scam:** Unofficial “guide” operators at Abbey Falls often charge ₹300‑₹500 for a “photo‑stop” without any official accreditation.  
- **Recommended approach:** Use the government‑run information kiosk at the entrance; hire a licensed guide only if you need a trek permit for Tadiandamol.

#### Madikeri food  
- **Signature dishes:** Pandi Curry (pork), Coorgi Kadubu (steamed rice dumplings), Nool Pitha (rice noodles), Bamboo Shoot Sambar.  
- **For toddlers and young kids:** Soft idli, plain boiled rice with dal, mashed banana pudding, butter‑roti (soft wheat roti).  
- **Vegetarian availability:** Most restaurants serve vegetarian versions of Kadubu and Nool Pitha; however, pork‑based dishes dominate menus, so ask for “vegetarian” explicitly.

## Bylakuppe (Tibetan Enclave)

**Key places:** Namdroling Monastery, Tibetan Handicraft Centre, Bylakuppe Market  

### Namdroling Monastery (Golden Temple)  
- Entry: **₹20 adult**, **₹10 child**; toddlers free but must be carried.  
- **Suitability:** Flat paved courtyard; **suitable for toddlers and elderly** (wheelchair‑friendly ramps).  
- **Best time:** Early morning (7:00‑9:00 am) for prayer chants and less crowd.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; open year‑round.

### Tibetan Handicraft Centre  
- Entry: **Free** (shopping only).  
- **Suitability:** Small indoor stalls; **suitable for all ages**.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑day (12:00‑2:00 pm) when artisans are present for demonstrations.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

#### Trust issue in Bylakuppe  
- **Scam:** Prayer‑flag bundles are often sold at double the market price with “authentic” certification.  
- **Recommended approach:** Purchase flags from the official monastery shop (₹5 per flag) or compare prices at the Handicraft Centre before buying.

#### Bylakuppe food  
- **Signature dishes:** Thukpa (Tibetan noodle soup), Veg Momo, Tingmo (steamed bread), Butter Tea.  
- **For toddlers and young kids:** Soft boiled noodles (plain thukpa), plain steamed rice with dal, butter tea with reduced salt.  
- **Vegetarian availability:** 100 % vegetarian; all Tibetan dishes are plant‑based, making it a safe zone for vegetarian travellers.

## Somwarpet (East Coorg)

**Key places:** Iruppu Falls, Chelavara Falls, Coffee Plantation, Somwarpet Market  

### Iruppu Falls (near Talakaveri)  
- Entry: **₹30 adult**, **₹15 child**; toddlers free but not allowed on wet rocks.  
- **Suitability:** 500 m trek with stone steps; **not suitable for toddlers**; elderly may need a walking stick.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (7:00‑9:00 am) before the sun intensifies the spray.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during monsoon (July‑September) due to flash‑flood risk.

### Chelavara Falls  
- Entry: **₹20 adult**, **₹10 child**; toddlers free but must be held.  
- **Suitability:** Short 200 m walk on a paved path; **suitable for toddlers and elderly** with a bench at the base.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00‑5:30 pm) when water flow is moderate.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None, but flow reduces sharply in December‑January.

### Coffee Plantation Tour (Somwarpet Estate)  
- Entry: **₹150 adult**, **₹80 child (5‑12 yr)**; toddlers free (no tasting).  
- **Suitability:** Flat walking trails through shade‑grown coffee bushes; **suitable for toddlers and elderly**.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑morning (10:00‑11:30 am) when the plantation is active and the temperature is comfortable.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed during heavy rains (June‑August).

### Somwarpet Market (weekly on Saturdays)  
- Entry: **Free**.  
- **Suitability:** Open‑air stalls; **suitable for all ages**.  
- **Best time:** 9:00‑12:00 am for fresh produce and less crowd.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

#### Trust issue in Somwarpet  
- **Scam:** Unlicensed “guided trek” operators at Iruppu Falls often demand advance cash without providing a written itinerary.  
- **Recommended approach:** Book treks through the Karnataka Forest Department office in Madikeri (₹250 per person) or use a reputable travel agency with online reviews.

#### Somwarpet food  
- **Signature dishes:** Coorgi Pandi Curry (pork), Coffee‑infused Biscuit, Akki Rotti (rice roti), Mushroom Thoran.  
- **For toddlers and young kids:** Soft boiled rice, plain dal, mashed potatoes, banana pancake (soft).  
- **Vegetarian availability:** Vegetarian versions of Akki Rotti and Mushroom Thoran are common; pork dishes dominate main‑course menus, so request “vegetarian” explicitly.

## General Coorg Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Cool (10‑22 °C), all attractions open, wildlife safaris most productive.  
- **April – June:** Warm (22‑30 °C), coffee estates in full bloom; early monsoon showers may begin.  
- **July – September:** Heavy monsoon; many waterfalls at peak flow but trekking routes (Tadiandamol, Iruppu) often closed; road landslides possible.  

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at **₹12 per km**; typical Madikeri‑Mysore run ≈ ₹1,200. Negotiate only for out‑station trips without a meter.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Fixed rates; Madikeri‑Bylakuppe ≈ ₹300; always confirm before boarding.  
- **App‑based options:** Ola and Uber operate in Madikeri and Mysore; expect surge pricing during festivals (e.g., Coorg Coffee Festival in November).  

**Dietary notes:**  
- **Dominant cuisine:** Coorgi (Kannada) with heavy use of pork, coconut, and local spices.  
- **Allergy risks:** Coconut oil in most curries; occasional use of dried red chilies.  
- **Vegetarian options:** Widely available in towns; rural eateries may focus on meat dishes, so ask for “vegetarian” or “no‑pork” explicitly.

**Medical:**  
- **Nearest quality hospital:** Cauvery Hospital, Madikeri (≈ 5 km from town centre, 24‑hour emergency).  
- **What to carry:** Basic first‑aid kit, any personal medication, a copy of health insurance, and a small stock of oral rehydration salts for children.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- **Nearest railway station:** Mysore Junction (≈ 120 km).  
- **Key trains from Delhi:** Karnataka Express (12627) and Rajdhani‑style **Kochu Express** (12623) both stop at Mysore.  
- **Key trains from Mumbai:** **Mysore – Mumbai Express** (16531) and **Deccan Express** (16533) arrive at Mysore.

**By air:**  
- **Nearest airport:** Mysore Airport (≈ 45 km from Madikeri).  
- **Airlines:** IndiGo, Air India, and SpiceJet operate daily flights from Bangalore (BLR) to Mysore (≈ ₹3,500‑₹5,000 one‑way).  
- **Approximate fare:** Bangalore‑Mysore ₹3,500‑₹5,000; Bangalore‑Coorg via private charter ≈ ₹12,000‑₹15,000.

**By road:**  
- **Key highway:** NH 275 (Bangalore‑Mysore‑Coorg).  
- **Nearest state bus depot:** Mysore KSRTC Bus Stand; frequent (every 30 min) buses to Madikeri (₹300‑₹400).  
- **Typical inter‑city bus fare:** Bangalore‑Madikeri ₹600‑₹800 (AC semi‑sleeper).

## Hidden Gems

**Chelavara Falls (near Somwarpet):**  
- **Why special:** A lesser‑known cascade with a shallow pool, ideal for a quiet picnic.  
- **Entry:** **₹20 adult**, **₹10 child**; toddlers free but must be supervised.  
- **How to reach:** From Somwarpet, take the SH 89 (30 km) and park at the designated pull‑out.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00‑5:30 pm) when water flow is gentle.  
- **Suitability:** Flat path, benches available – **suitable for toddlers and elderly**.

**Kumara Parvatha Viewpoint (near Tadiandamol):**  
- **Why special:** Offers a panoramic vista of the Western Ghats without the full trek to the summit.  
- **Entry:** **Free** (parking ₹30 per vehicle).  
- **How to reach:** Drive up SH 88 to the “Kumara Parvatha” parking lot (≈ 12 km from Madikeri).  
- **Best time:** Sunrise (5:30‑6:30 am) for mist‑filled valleys.  
- **Suitability:** Short 200 m walk on a graded path – **suitable for elderly; toddlers can be carried**.

**Iluppur (Iruppu) River Rafting (Talakaveri stretch):**  
- **Why special:** Controlled rafting on the Kaveri’s upper reaches, rarely crowded.  
- **Entry:** **₹1,200 per adult**, **₹800 per child (7‑12 yr)**; toddlers not allowed for safety.  
- **How to reach:** Book via the Karnataka Forest Department office in Madikeri; they arrange pickup from Talacauvery.  
- **Best time:** Post‑monsoon (October‑December) when water is brisk but manageable.  
- **Suitability:** **Not suitable for toddlers or elderly with mobility issues**; requires basic fitness.

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio and Airtel provide strong 4G coverage in Madikeri and Bylakuppe; Vi has spotty service in remote valleys (e.g., near Tadiandamol). Expect intermittent loss of signal on steep mountain roads.

**ATMs:**  
- SBI and HDFC ATMs are located near Madikeri Market and the Government Hospital. Bylakuppe has a State Bank of India ATM at the monastery’s entrance. Carry some cash (₹2,000‑₹5,000) as many small eateries accept only cash.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase a fresh SIM at Bangalore Airport (Jio or Airtel kiosks) for immediate activation. In Coorg, authorized Jio stores operate in Madikeri and Mysore; Airtel has a franchise at the Mysore bus depot. Avoid street‑vendor offers that skip the mandatory passport‑photo paperwork.
