# Delhi Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: New Delhi → Old Delhi → South Delhi**

The circuit starts at New Delhi Railway Station (central hub) because it offers easy metro and taxi access to the historic core (Old Delhi). From Old Delhi, the narrow lanes and pedestrian‑only streets are best explored on foot before moving south where major highways (NH‑48) allow faster travel to spacious parks and modern temples. This order minimizes back‑tracking, reduces traffic fatigue, and lets families with toddlers rest in the greener South Delhi sites after the bustling Old Delhi market walk.

Driving times:  
- New Delhi → Old Delhi: 0.5 hours (via Mahatma Gandhi Road, NH‑44)  
- Old Delhi → South Delhi: 0.75 hours (via Ring Road / NH‑48)

**Alternative routing:** Fly into IGI Airport, take the Delhi Metro Airport Express to New Delhi, then follow the same sequence; this avoids city‑center congestion for first‑time visitors.

## Old Delhi

**Key places:** Red Fort, Jama Masjid, Chandni Chowk, Gurudwara Bangla Sahib, Jantar Mantar  

**Red Fort:**  
- Entry: ₹35 adult, ₹20 child (5‑15 yr) – free for seniors (≥60 yr) and students with ID.  
- Suitability: 1.2 km of paved pathways; steep stairs to the museum section – **not suitable for toddlers or mobility‑limited elders**.  
- Best time: Early morning (08:00‑10:00) to avoid heat and crowds.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Fridays for maintenance; open all other days.

**Jama Masjid:**  
- Entry: Free; minaret climb ₹20 adult, ₹10 child.  
- Suitability: Flat courtyard is stroller‑friendly, but the minaret’s narrow spiral is **unsuitable for toddlers and elders**.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (16:00‑18:00) when prayer crowds thin.  
- Seasonal closure: None; prayer schedule may restrict entry during Jumu’ah.

**Gurudwara Bangla Sahib:**  
- Entry: Free (donations welcome).  
- Suitability: Fully accessible ramps and quiet prayer hall – **suitable for toddlers and elders**; shoes must be removed.  
- Best time: Early morning (07:00‑09:00) for the serene “kirtan” session.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Jantar Mantar:**  
- Entry: ₹10 adult, ₹5 child (≤12 yr).  
- Suitability: Large open‑air instrument field with smooth walkways – **suitable for toddlers (under supervision) and elders**.  
- Best time: Mid‑day (11:00‑13:00) when the sun casts clear shadows for photography.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays for cleaning.

**Chandni Chowk (market):**  
- Entry: Free (shopping expenses separate).  
- Suitability: Narrow lanes; **not stroller‑friendly**; elders may find the crowds taxing.  
- Best time: Early morning (07:00‑09:00) before the market peaks.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Scam/trust issue for this area:**  
- **Auto‑rickshaw overcharging:** Drivers often quote inflated flat rates for short hops (e.g., ₹150 for a 2 km ride).  
- Recommended approach: Insist on the meter or negotiate a fare of ₹20‑₹30 per km before boarding; use app‑based rides (Ola/Uber) for transparent pricing.

**Old Delhi food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Kebabs*, *Paratha* with *Aloo* stuffing, *Jalebi*, *Chole Bhature*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft *Idli* with plain *Dahi*, plain *Rice* with *Dal* (no spices), *Aloo Paratha* (mild).  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most street stalls are purely vegetarian, though some kebab stalls serve meat.

## South Delhi

**Key places:** Qutub Minar, Humayun’s Tomb, Lodi Garden, Akshardham Temple, Garden of Five Senses  

**Qutub Minar:**  
- Entry: ₹40 adult, ₹20 child (≤12 yr).  
- Suitability: 1.5 km of paved walk; steep steps to the top – **not suitable for toddlers or elders with limited mobility**.  
- Best time: Sunrise (06:00‑08:00) for soft light and fewer visitors.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Fridays for prayer.

**Humayun’s Tomb:**  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (≤12 yr).  
- Suitability: Wide lawns and ramps; **suitable for toddlers (stroller‑friendly) and elders**.  
- Best time: Late morning (10:00‑12:00) when the marble glows.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Lodi Garden:**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Flat, well‑maintained paths, plenty of shade – **ideal for toddlers and elders**.  
- Best time: Early evening (17:00‑19:00) for a cool breeze.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Akshardham Temple:**  
- Entry: Free; cultural‑exhibit tickets ₹100 adult, ₹50 child (≤12 yr).  
- Suitability: Large marble complex with ramps; **suitable for toddlers (except the water show) and elders**.  
- Best time: Evening (18:00‑20:00) for the musical fountain show.  
- Seasonal closure: None; the water show is cancelled during heavy rain.

**Garden of Five Senses:**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Gentle walking trails, sensory gardens – **perfect for toddlers and elders**.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (16:00‑18:00) when the garden’s scent installations are most active.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Scam/trust issue for this area:**  
- **Ticket scalping at Qutub Minar:** Unauthorized vendors sell “fast‑track” tickets at ₹300‑₹500.  
- Recommended approach: Purchase tickets only at the official counter or via the Delhi Tourism website; avoid third‑party agents.

**South Delhi food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Butter Chicken*, *Dal Makhani*, *Paneer Tikka*, *Chaat* (Aloo Tikki).  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft *Dosa* with plain *Coconut Chutney*, *Plain Paratha* with *Ghee*, *Rice* with *Mild Dal*.  
- Vegetarian availability: Very high; most restaurants offer a full veg menu; meat dishes are clearly labeled.

## North Delhi

**Key places:** National Zoological Park, Dilli Haat, National Museum, Agrasen ki Baoli, Mehrauli Archaeological Park  

**National Zoological Park:**  
- Entry: ₹200 adult, ₹100 child (5‑12 yr).  
- Suitability: 5 km of paved trails; wheel‑chair‑friendly pathways – **suitable for toddlers (stroller‑compatible) and elders**.  
- Best time: Early morning (09:00‑11:00) when animals are active.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Dilli Haat (INA):**  
- Entry: Free; individual stall purchases vary.  
- Suitability: Open‑air craft market with flat walkways – **suitable for toddlers and elders**.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (16:00‑19:00) to enjoy live folk performances.  
- Seasonal closure: None; indoor craft hall may close during heavy rain.

**National Museum:**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (≤12 yr).  
- Suitability: Elevator access; **suitable for toddlers (short visits) and elders**.  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12:00‑14:00) when guided tours are available.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Agrasen ki Baoli (step‑well):**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Narrow stone steps, dim lighting – **not suitable for toddlers or elders with mobility issues**.  
- Best time: Early evening (17:00‑19:00) for atmospheric lighting.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Mehrauli Archaeological Park:**  
- Entry: Free.  
- Suitability: Uneven terrain, scattered ruins – **not ideal for toddlers; elders should use a walking stick**.  
- Best time: Sunrise (06:00‑08:00) for photography.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Scam/trust issue for this area:**  
- **“Free entry” bait at the zoo:** Some vendors claim the zoo is free and then charge for “special animal feeding” tours at inflated rates.  
- Recommended approach: Stick to the official ticket; any extra activity should be booked at the zoo’s information desk with clear pricing.

**North Delhi food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Kadhi‑Chawal*, *Chole Puri*, *Methi Thepla*, *Mutton Korma*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft *Khichdi* with *Ghee*, *Plain Roti* with *Butter*, *Yogurt* (plain).  
- Vegetarian availability: Good; most street stalls serve veg dishes; meat is limited to specific stalls.

## General Delhi Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – February:** Cool (8‑20 °C), major festivals (Diwali, Holi) – all attractions open, outdoor gardens pleasant.  
- **March – May:** Hot (30‑45 °C); early morning visits recommended; some outdoor sites (Lodi Garden) can be uncomfortable midday.  
- **June – September:** Monsoon; occasional water‑logging at Old Delhi markets; indoor museums (National Museum) remain fully accessible.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at ₹40 for the first km, then ₹12‑₹15 per km; night surcharge + ₹30.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Base fare ₹25 for the first km, then ₹12 per km; negotiate before boarding; app‑auto (Ola Auto) shows exact fare.  
- **App‑based options:** Uber and Ola dominate; typical city‑wide ride ₹120‑₹250 for a 10 km trip; share‑ride discounts available.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: North Indian (wheat‑based rotis, dairy‑rich gravies).  
- Allergy risks: Heavy use of ghee and butter; occasional peanuts in chaat; coconut oil rarely used in Delhi but appears in some South‑Indian stalls.  
- Vegetarian options: Abundant; most street food and restaurant menus have a dedicated veg section; meat dishes are clearly labeled.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest top‑tier hospital: **AIIMS (All India Institute of Medical Sciences), New Delhi** – 5 km from India Gate, 10 km from Old Delhi.  
- Recommended to carry: Basic first‑aid kit, oral rehydration salts, any prescription meds, and a copy of health insurance. Emergency number: 112.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- Nearest major station: **New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS)** – 2 km from Connaught Place.  
- Key trains from Delhi: **Rajdhani Express (12301)** to Kolkata, **Shatabdi Express (12001)** to Chandigarh – both stop at NDLS.  

**By air:**  
- Nearest airport: **Indira Gandhi International Airport (IGI)** – 16 km from central Delhi.  
- Airlines: Air India, IndiGo, Vistara, SpiceJet.  
- Approximate one‑way fare (from major Indian metros): ₹3,000 – ₹8,000, depending on advance booking and season.

**By road:**  
- Main highway: **NH‑48 (Delhi‑Gurgaon Expressway)** connects to the city’s Ring Road network.  
- Nearest state bus depot: **ISBT Kashmiri Gate** – services to neighboring states.  
- Inter‑city bus frequency: Every 30 minutes; typical fare to Jaipur ₹700‑₹900.

## Hidden Gems

**Agrasen ki Baoli (step‑well):**  
- Why special: A 15th‑century step‑well hidden behind a modest gate; rarely crowded, offers a quiet, photogenic spot.  
- Entry: Free; reach via a short walk from Connaught Place.  
- Best time: Early evening (17:00‑19:00) when the stone glows in the sunset.  
- Suitability: Narrow steps make it **unsuitable for toddlers and elders with mobility concerns**.

**Garden of Five Senses:**  
- Why special: Themed gardens (herbs, bamboo, desert) designed for sensory immersion; perfect for families seeking a relaxed stroll away from traffic.  
- Entry: Free; located near Saket.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (16:00‑18:00) when the fragrance installations are active.  
- Suitability: Flat, wheelchair‑friendly paths – **ideal for toddlers and elders**.

**Sanjay Van (forest reserve):**  
- Why special: A 445‑acre forest on the edge of Delhi, offering bird‑watching trails and a peaceful escape from the city buzz.  
- Entry: Free; entry gate at the Delhi‑Gurgaon border.  
- Best time: Early morning (06:00‑09:00) for wildlife sightings.  
- Suitability: Well‑marked trails, but some uneven sections – **moderately suitable for toddlers (use carrier) and elders (use walking stick).**

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide 4G/5G coverage across most of Delhi; metro tunnels often have weak signal, so keep a portable hotspot.  

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs at **SBI (Connaught Place), HDFC (Kashmiri Gate), and ICICI (South Delhi)**; cash is preferred in street markets, but cards are accepted in most restaurants and malls.  

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at IGI Airport’s authorized Jio/Airtel kiosks (₹500 – ₹800 for a 28‑day prepaid pack).  
- Jio offers the best overall coverage in Delhi, especially in the Old Delhi narrow lanes.
