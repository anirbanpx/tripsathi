# Kolkata Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Howrah Railway Station → Esplanade (Central Kolkata) → South Kolkata (Eco Park)**  

The sequence starts at Howrah, the main rail gateway, because the Howrah Bridge offers a quick 15‑minute crossing onto the city’s historic core (Esplanade). From there you can walk or take a short auto‑rickshaw ride to the Victoria Memorial and Indian Museum. Continuing south along the Eastern Metropolitan Bypass (EM Bypass, NH 12) brings you to Eco Park and the Rabindra Sarobar lake area, allowing you to avoid the congested inner‑city traffic and finish the day in a greener setting.

Driving times:  
- Howrah Railway Station → Esplanade: 0.5 h (NH 16 via Howrah Bridge)  
- Esplanade → Eco Park (Ballygunge): 1.0 h (EM Bypass, NH 12)  

**Alternative routing:** Arrive via Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport, take the VIP road to Salt Lake, then cross the Vidyasagar Setu to reach South Kolkata first; this is useful if you plan a weekend trip to the Sundarbans after the city tour.

## North Kolkata

**Key places:** College Street, Jorasanko Thakur Bari, Metcalfe Hall, Marble Palace, Kumartuli

**College Street (Boi Para):**  
- Entry: Free (no ticket)  
- Suitability: Fully suitable for toddlers and elderly; flat pavement but can be crowded during exam season, so use a stroller carrier.  
- Best time of day: Early morning (7‑9 am) for cooler air and quieter aisles.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Jorasanko Thakur Bari (Tagore House):**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (3–12 yr)  
- Suitability: 2 km of uneven stone pathways; not recommended for toddlers without a carrier; elderly may need a walking stick.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4‑5 pm) when guided tours start.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays.

**Metcalfe Hall:**  
- Entry: Free (donations welcome)  
- Suitability: Wide steps and a spacious courtyard – ideal for toddlers to run safely; wheelchair‑accessible ramp installed in 2023.  
- Best time: Mid‑morning (10‑11 am) for optimal natural light for photography.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Marble Palace:**  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (3–12 yr)  
- Suitability: 1 km of marble corridors with occasional steep stairs; not toddler‑friendly unless you can lift the child. Elderly visitors should avoid the upper gallery due to narrow staircases.  
- Best time: Early afternoon (1‑2 pm) when the house is less crowded.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Kumartuli (Potters’ Quarter):**  
- Entry: Free (donations appreciated)  
- Suitability: Narrow lanes; toddlers should be carried; elderly may find the uneven cobbles tiring.  
- Best time: Evening (5‑7 pm) when artisans are polishing idols.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for North Kolkata:**  
- **Guide overcharging at College Street:** Unlicensed “tour guides” often approach tourists near the university and demand ₹500‑₹800 for a 30‑minute walk.  
- Recommended approach: Insist on a licensed guide from the West Bengal Tourism Development Corporation (WBTC) office at Esplanade; their fee is a transparent ₹200 per hour.

**North Kolkata food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Kathi rolls* (Kolkata roll), *Macher Jhal* (spicy fish), *Mishti Doi* (sweet yogurt), *Rasgulla*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft *idli* with plain *dal*, *plain rice with ghee and a spoon of mashed potatoes*, *sweet‑mild *pulao* (vegetable rice).  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most street stalls and restaurants serve pure‑veg options, though some fish dishes dominate the menu.

## Central Kolkata

**Key places:** Victoria Memorial, Indian Museum, St. Paul’s Cathedral, Mother House (Loreto), Science City

**Victoria Memorial:**  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (3–12 yr); ₹5 for senior citizens (60+).  
- Suitability: Large lawns and ramps; fully stroller‑compatible and wheelchair‑friendly.  
- Best time: Sunrise (6‑7 am) for pastel lighting; also quieter on weekdays.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but the museum closes at 5 pm during monsoon (June‑September).

**Indian Museum:**  
- Entry: ₹25 adult, ₹10 child (3–12 yr); free for students with ID.  
- Suitability: Two‑storey building with elevators; toddlers can enjoy the dinosaur exhibit but need supervision on the upper floors. Elderly may prefer the ground‑floor galleries.  
- Best time: Late morning (11 am‑12 pm) before the crowd peaks.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**St. Paul’s Cathedral:**  
- Entry: Free (donations accepted)  
- Suitability: Flat interior, narrow pews; suitable for toddlers (carry on lap) and elderly (no steps).  
- Best time: Early afternoon (2‑3 pm) when organ music is played.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Mother House (Loreto):**  
- Entry: Free (guided tours ₹50 adult, ₹25 child)  
- Suitability: Small chapel with a single flight of stairs; not ideal for toddlers unless carried; elderly may need assistance on the stairs.  
- Best time: Morning (9‑10 am) when the convent is quiet.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Sundays for worship.

**Science City:**  
- Entry: ₹100 adult, ₹50 child (3–12 yr); ₹30 senior (60+).  
- Suitability: Fully accessible; the 3‑D Digital Dome has a ramp, but the “Space Theatre” has a steep ramp that may challenge very elderly travellers.  
- Best time: Late afternoon (4‑5 pm) to catch the laser show.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but the outdoor exhibits may be closed during heavy rain (July‑August).

**Trust issue or pricing trap for Central Kolkata:**  
- **Ticket scalping at Victoria Memorial:** Vendors near the gate sometimes sell “fast‑track” tickets at ₹150‑₹200, promising skip‑the‑line entry.  
- Recommended approach: Purchase tickets online via the official website (victoria‑memorial.gov.in) or at the museum’s official counter; the official price is ₹30.

**Central Kolkata food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Puchka* (Bengali panipuri), *Kosha Mangsho* (spicy mutton), *Aloo Posto* (potatoes with poppy seeds), *Sandesh* (sweet).  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft *dal‑puri* (lentil stuffed flatbread), *plain boiled rice with a dab of ghee*, *mild vegetable curry* (no chilies).  
- Vegetarian availability: Very strong; most eateries have a dedicated veg menu; fish is optional.

## South Kolkata

**Key places:** Howrah Bridge, Eco Park, Birla Planetarium, Rabindra Sarobar, Tram Museum

**Howrah Bridge (Rabindra Setu):**  
- Entry: Free (no ticket)  
- Suitability: Pedestrian walkway is wide and level; perfect for toddlers on a stroller and elderly walkers.  
- Best time: Early evening (6‑7 pm) for sunset views and cooler breeze.  
- Seasonal closure: None.

**Eco Park:**  
- Entry: ₹30 adult, ₹15 child (3–12 yr); free for children under 3.  
- Suitability: Flat, well‑maintained pathways; stroller‑friendly. The “Toy Train” (₹250) has a short 30‑minute ride with a gentle slope, suitable for toddlers.  
- Best time: Morning (9‑11 am) to avoid the afternoon heat.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but boat rides (₹200 per boat) are suspended during heavy monsoon.

**Birla Planetarium:**  
- Entry: ₹50 adult, ₹25 child (3–12 yr); ₹30 senior.  
- Suitability: Wheelchair‑accessible entrance; the dome’s seating is sloped, so toddlers should be held on laps. Elderly may need assistance to reach the lower rows.  
- Best time: Evening shows (7‑8 pm) for the “Night Sky” program.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Mondays.

**Rabindra Sarobar (Lake):**  
- Entry: Free (boat ride ₹150 per hour)  
- Suitability: Flat walking track around the lake; ideal for stroller walks and gentle jogging for seniors.  
- Best time: Early morning (6‑8 am) for misty reflections.  
- Seasonal closure: None, but boat services stop during heavy rain.

**Tram Museum (Kolkata Tramways):**  
- Entry: ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (3–12 yr)  
- Suitability: Small indoor space with a single ramp; suitable for toddlers (carry) and elderly (ramp).  
- Best time: Mid‑day (12‑2 pm) when the museum is least crowded.  
- Seasonal closure: Closed on Tuesdays.

**Trust issue or pricing trap for South Kolkata:**  
- **Eco Park “toy‑train” over‑charging:** Unofficial vendors near the entrance may ask for ₹350 instead of the official ₹250.  
- Recommended approach: Use the official ticket counter inside the park; keep the receipt for any refunds.

**South Kolkata food:**  
- Signature dishes: *Macher Muro* (fish in mustard sauce), *Chingri Macher Malai Curry* (prawn in coconut milk), *Pithe* (sweet rice cakes), *Misti Doi*.  
- For toddlers and young kids: Soft *steamed rice* with a spoon of *dal* (no spices), *plain boiled potatoes*, *mild vegetable pulao*.  
- Vegetarian availability: Good; many restaurants offer *Paneer Bhapa* (steamed cottage cheese) and *Aloo Gobi*.

## General Kolkata Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Pleasant winter (15‑25 °C), all attractions open; festivals like Durga Puja (Sept‑Oct) bring crowds but vibrant cultural shows.  
- **April – June:** Pre‑monsoon heat (30‑35 °C); early mornings are comfortable; some outdoor museums (Science City) may have limited shade.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon; heavy showers can close boat rides at Eco Park and cause traffic jams; indoor attractions (Museums, Planetarium) remain fully operational.  

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at ₹25 for the first km, then ₹13 per km; night surcharge (10 % after 10 pm). Negotiating is common for airport trips; insist on meter or pre‑agreed fare (≈₹350 – ₹400).  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Flag‑down fare ₹15 for the first km, then ₹8 per km; always ask for “meter” (many autos are still unmetered) or agree on ₹30‑₹40 for short city hops.  
- **App‑based options:** Uber, Ola, and Rapido (bike‑share) operate city‑wide; UberX starts at ₹120 for a 5 km ride.  

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Bengali – heavy use of mustard oil, poppy seeds, and fish; coconut oil appears in coastal dishes.  
- Allergy risks: Groundnut oil in street snacks; some sweets contain milk solids (lactose).  
- Vegetarian options: Very strong; most restaurants have a pure‑veg menu; however, “egg” is often considered non‑veg by locals, so check if you need a strict veg diet.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: **Apollo Gleneagles Hospital**, 2 km from Esplanade (Park Street area). 24‑hour emergency, English‑speaking staff.  
- What to carry: Basic first‑aid kit, any prescription meds, a copy of health insurance, and a small bottle of oral rehydration salts (ORS) for travelers’ diarrhea.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- Nearest railway station: **Howrah Junction** (≈2 km from central Kolkata). Major trains: Howrah – New Delhi Rajdhani (12431/12432), Howrah – Mumbai Duronto (12261/12262), Howrah – Chennai Coromandel Express (12841/12842).  

**By air:**  
- Nearest airport: **Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport (CCU)**, 17 km north of city centre. Airlines: Air India, IndiGo, SpiceJet, Vistara. Approximate one‑way fare from Delhi: ₹3,500 – ₹5,500; from Mumbai: ₹4,000 – ₹6,000.  

**By road:**  
- Key highways: **NH 16** (Kolkata‑Chennai) and **NH 12** (Kolkata‑Siliguri).  
- Nearest state bus depot: **Kolkata Central Bus Stand (Esplanade)**, with frequent inter‑city buses to Delhi, Bangalore, and Guwahati. Typical fare to Delhi: ₹1,200 – ₹1,500; to Guwahati: ₹800 – ₹1,000.

## Hidden Gems

**Kumartuli’s Night Workshop:**  
- Uncrowded alleys where artisans finish idol painting after sunset; you can watch the delicate brushwork up close.  
- No entry fee; reach via a short walk from Kumartuli lane; best visited 8‑10 pm.  
- Suitability: Carry toddlers; elderly may find the dim lighting challenging.

**RBI Museum (Reserve Bank of India):**  
- Small museum inside the RBI building showcasing India’s monetary history; often missed by mainstream guides.  
- Entry: Free (security check required). Open 10 am‑4 pm on weekdays.  
- Best time: Mid‑morning to avoid the lunch crowd.  
- Suitability: Flat interior, wheelchair‑friendly; toddlers can be carried.

**Maidan Heritage Walk (Maidan‑Park area):**  
- A 2‑km guided walk covering colonial statues, the historic Eden Gardens perimeter, and the hidden “Maidan Club” garden.  
- Entry: ₹50 per person (guided).  
- Best time: Early evening (5‑6 pm) when the park lights are on.  
- Suitability: Even paths, good for strollers; elderly may enjoy the benches along the route.

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio, Airtel, and Vi provide 4G coverage across most of Kolkata; however, the historic lanes of North Kolkata (e.g., College Street) can have patchy signal. Carry a portable charger for extended walks.

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs: SBI (Esplanade), HDFC (Park Street), ICICI (Garia). Most tourist zones accept cards, but small street vendors often prefer cash; keep ₹500‑₹1,000 in small denominations for snacks and tips.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at the airport’s official **Airtel** or **Jio** kiosks (₹500 prepaid with ₹200 credit). For better coverage in suburban and river‑crossing areas, Jio’s network is recommended. Avoid “black‑market” SIM sellers who skip the mandatory documentation and may give you a non‑verified SIM that gets deactivated after a few days.
