# Thekkady / Periyar Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Kochi → Kottayam → Thekkady**

Kochi is the nearest international airport and major rail hub; reaching Kottayam first lets you join NH 544, a well‑maintained four‑lane highway. From Kottayam you switch to NH 183 (Kottayam‑Kumily road) that climbs into the highlands, allowing a gradual ascent and avoiding the steep, winding stretches that begin after Kumily. This sequence reduces driver fatigue and ensures you have fuel and food options before the forest‑edge stretch.

Driving times:  
- Kochi → Kottayam: 1.5 hours (NH 544)  
- Kottayam → Thekkady: 3.5 hours (NH 183 via Kumily)

**Alternative routing:** Arrive via Coimbatore (NH 44) and take the Munnar‑Periyar road (SH 19). This adds about 30 minutes but offers scenic tea‑garden views; useful if you are already touring the Western Ghats from Tamil Nadu.

## Thekkady Town

**Key places:** Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary, Thekkady Boat Jetty, Elephant Camp, Kadathanadan Kalari Centre, local market

**Periyar Boat Safari:**  
- Entry: ₹150 adult, ₹100 child (5–12 yr) – free for toddlers under 5 (must be held).  
- Suitability: 2 km calm lake; **not suitable for toddlers** who cannot sit unaided, and **not ideal for elderly with limited mobility** due to boat steps.  
- Best time of day: 7:30 am–9:30 am (early light and animal activity).  
- Seasonal closure: Fully suspended during heavy monsoon (mid‑June to early July) for safety.

**Elephant Camp & Safari:**  
- Entry: ₹250 adult, ₹150 child (5–12 yr).  
- Suitability: **Unsuitable for toddlers** (risk of falls) and **elderly with joint issues** (uneven terrain, steep ramps).  
- Best time: 10:00 am–12:00 pm (cooler, elephants feed).  
- Seasonal note: Camp open year‑round; rides may be limited in July‑August due to rain.

**Trust issue – Boat Safari overcharging:**  
- Some private operators inflate private‑boat rates to ₹500 per person and claim “exclusive wildlife view”.  
- Recommended approach: Purchase tickets only at the official Forest Department counter (near the jetty) and verify the printed ticket’s serial number.

**Thekkady food:**  
- Signature dishes: Kerala Sadya (banana‑leaf feast), Appam with Kerala fish stew, Kerala chicken roast.  
- For toddlers and young kids: soft idli, plain rice with dal, mashed banana with a drizzle of ghee.  
- Vegetarian availability: Very high; most restaurants serve a full vegetarian Sadya and thoran, avial, and banana‑leaf veg plates.

## Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary & Spice Plantation Zone

**Key places:** Periyar Tiger Reserve, Spice Plantation (cardamom, pepper, vanilla), Kadathanadan Traditional House, Kallar River viewpoint

**Spice Plantation Guided Tour:**  
- Entry: ₹200 adult, ₹120 child (5–12 yr).  
- Suitability: 1.5 km of uneven paths with occasional steps; **not recommended for toddlers** and **elderly with walking difficulties**.  
- Best time of day: 10:30 am–12:30 pm (when leaves are glossy and guides can point out active harvesting).  
- Seasonal note: Pepper harvest peaks Nov‑Feb; tours are richer then, but the trail remains open year‑round.

**Kadathanadan Kalari Demonstration:**  
- Entry: ₹100 adult, ₹50 child (under 12).  
- Suitability: Indoor arena, **suitable for toddlers** (seated viewing) and **elderly** (no walking required).  
- Best time: Late afternoon (cooler, performances scheduled).  
- Seasonal closure: None, but performances may be canceled during heavy rain.

**Trust issue – Spice “organic” premium trap:**  
- Vendors often quote “organic” prices up to 40 % higher without certification, targeting tourists.  
- Recommended approach: Insist on a government‑run plantation tour (ticketed at the forest office) or ask to see the organic certification before paying.

**Periyar food:**  
- Signature dishes: Kerala fish molee (coconut‑based), beef fry with Kerala spices, tapioca with fish curry.  
- For toddlers and young kids: soft appam with plain coconut milk, boiled sweet potato mash, plain rice with ghee and a spoon of curd.  
- Vegetarian availability: Excellent; most stalls serve plantain chips, thoran, and a vegetable Sadya version.

## General Thekkady / Periyar Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Cool (15‑25 °C), dry; all wildlife activities open, boat safaris run daily.  
- **April – May:** Warm (28‑35 °C); early mornings pleasant, but midday heat can be intense—carry water and sun protection.  
- **June – September:** Monsoon (heavy rain, 200‑300 mm); boat safaris often halted (mid‑June to early July), trails become slippery—ideal for off‑season budget travelers but require rain gear.

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi from Kochi Airport to Thekkady:** ₹3,500‑4,000 (fixed‑rate; negotiate before boarding, meter not used).  
- **Local auto‑rickshaw:** ₹15 per km; for trips beyond 5 km, agree on a flat rate (e.g., ₹250 for a round trip to the boat jetty).  
- **App‑based options:** Uber/Ola operate in Kochi but not reliably in Thekkady; local app “Kerala Taxi” offers ₹2,200‑2,800 for a 4‑hour day hire with driver.

**Dietary notes:**  
- Dominant cuisine: Coconut‑rich Kerala fare; expect coconut oil, grated coconut, and red chilies in most dishes.  
- Allergy risks: Coconut (oil or grated), mustard seeds, and occasional raw red chili.  
- Pork is uncommon in mainstream Kerala restaurants but may appear in Christian‑run eateries; always ask if you avoid it.  
- Vegetarian options: Abundant; almost every meal can be made vegetarian on request, with ample lentil, vegetable, and rice dishes.

**Medical:**  
- Nearest quality hospital: Government Medical College, Kottayam (≈ 80 km, 2‑hour drive). Private facilities in Kumily (Kumily Medical Centre, 5 km from town) offer basic emergency care.  
- Recommended to carry: personal prescription meds, basic first‑aid kit, antidiarrheal tablets, insect repellent (DEET ≥ 30 %), and a small stock of oral rehydration salts for children.
