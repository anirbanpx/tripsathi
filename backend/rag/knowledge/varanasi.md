# Varanasi Travel Knowledge

## Routing

**Standard routing: Delhi → Varanasi → Sarnath → Ramnagar**

- **Why this order?** Delhi is the most common entry point (air, rail, or highway). Starting with Varanasi city lets travellers adjust to the bustling ghats and get a feel for the spiritual atmosphere. Sarnath lies 10 km north of the city and is best visited after a night in Varanasi to avoid early‑morning crowd rushes. Ramnagar is on the opposite (west) bank of the Ganga, reachable after Sarnath without back‑tracking, and its fort is best seen in the late afternoon when the light softens.

Driving times:  
- Delhi → Varanasi: **12–13 hrs** (NH 19, formerly NH 2)  
- Varanasi → Sarnath: **30 min** (NH 31)  
- Sarnath → Ramnagar: **1 hr** (NH 31 / 31A)  

**Alternative routing:** Fly into Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport, head straight to Sarnath for a sunrise visit, then cross the Ganga to the city core; or take a loop via Chunar Fort (≈ 45 km north) before returning to Varanasi for a day‑trip perspective.

## Varanasi City Core

**Key places:** Kashi Vishwanath Temple, Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple, Durga Temple (Madan Mohan), Manikarnika Ghat, Dashashwamedh Ghat, Assi Ghat, Tulsi Ghat, Bharat Kala Bhavan (BHU Museum)

### Kashi Vishwanath Temple
- **Entry:** Free for darshan; **Rudrabhishek** ₹1,100 – ₹2,100, **Sparsh Darshan** ₹500, **Mangala Aarti** ₹200 (online booking required 30 days in advance).  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: manageable – temple steps are steep; use the ramp at the main entrance. *Toddlers*: not recommended for inside the sanctum due to crowd and security checks.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (4:00 am – 6:00 am) for Mangala Aarti; otherwise 9:00 am – 12:00 pm for regular darshan.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None; temple remains open year‑round.

### Sankat Mochan Hanuman Temple
- **Entry:** Free; optional donation ₹20.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: easy flat ground, wheelchair‑friendly entrance. *Toddlers*: can sit on parents’ lap; no steep steps.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00 pm – 6:00 pm) when the **Evening Aarti** is performed.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Durga Temple (Madan Mohan)
- **Entry:** Free; donation ₹30 suggested.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: narrow corridors, but manageable with assistance. *Toddlers*: not ideal – the inner sanctum is crowded and the deity’s platform is raised.  
- **Best time:** Morning (8:00 am – 10:00 am) for a quieter visit.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Manikarnika Ghat (Funeral Ghat)
- **Entry:** Free; boat ride ₹150 – ₹200 per person (30 min).  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: boat ride is safe; walking along the ghats can be slippery—use a sturdy walking stick. *Toddlers*: boat ride is fine, but the surrounding cremation area may be distressing; keep child close.  
- **Best time:** Dawn (5:30 am – 7:00 am) for the iconic sunrise and incense‑filled atmosphere.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None, but monsoon can make the steps slippery.

### Dashashwamedh Ghat
- **Entry:** Free; boat ride ₹150 – ₹200.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: boat ride eliminates the need to climb; on‑shore walk is moderate (≈ 200 m). *Toddlers*: boat ride is enjoyable; avoid peak crowd hours (10:00 am‑12:00 pm).  
- **Best time:** Evening (5:30 pm – 7:00 pm) for the **Ganga Aarti**.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Assi Ghat
- **Entry:** Free.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: gentle slope, benches available. *Toddlers*: safe to sit on the steps; early morning (6:00 am) is calm.  
- **Best time:** Sunrise (5:30 am – 7:00 am) for yoga and quiet prayers.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Tulsi Ghat
- **Entry:** Free; cultural shows ₹100 – ₹250 (if attending).  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: flat promenade, wheelchair‑accessible. *Toddlers*: can enjoy the street‑performers; keep within sight as crowds can surge during festivals.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00 pm – 6:00 pm) when classical music sessions start.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Bharat Kala Bhavan (BHU Museum)
- **Entry:** ₹20 adult, ₹10 child (3‑12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: air‑conditioned, ramps available. *Toddlers*: short galleries; stroller‑friendly but avoid the sculpture hall with steep steps.  
- **Best time:** Mid‑morning (10:00 am – 12:00 pm) to avoid museum crowd.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Mondays and during major festivals (e.g., Diwali).

**Trust issue – Temple & Ghat scams:**  
- **Scam:** Unauthorized “ticket agents” at the temple entrance ask for extra “fast‑track” fees (₹500‑₹1,000) and claim they guarantee a front‑row darshan.  
- **Recommended approach:** Use the official online portal (https://kashivishwanath.org) or the on‑site ticket counter. Do not pay any extra cash to street vendors.

**City Core food:**  
- **Signature dishes:** *Kachori Sabzi*, *Banarasi paan*, *Tamatar Chaat*, *Litti Chokha*, *Thandai*.  
- **For toddlers & young kids:** *Plain idli*, *soft boiled rice with dal*, *khichdi*, *steamed carrot‑potato mash*.  
- **Vegetarian availability:** Very high; most street stalls are pure‑veg. Non‑veg (e.g., kebabs) is limited to specific restaurants.

## Sarnath (North of Varanasi)

**Key places:** Dhamek Stupa, Sarnath Museum, Mulagandha Kuti Vihar, Ashoka Pillar, Archaeological Museum

### Dhamek Stupa
- **Entry:** Free; optional donation ₹50.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: flat surrounding garden, benches available. *Toddlers*: open space to run, but keep close to the railings.  
- **Best time:** Early morning (6:30 am – 8:00 am) for soft light and fewer tourists.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Sarnath Museum (Archaeological Museum)
- **Entry:** ₹50 adult, ₹25 child (3‑12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: wheelchair‑friendly entrance, air‑conditioned. *Toddlers*: short exhibits; avoid the upper gallery with steep stairs.  
- **Best time:** Late morning (11:00 am – 1:00 pm) before the heat peaks.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Mondays.

### Mulagandha Kuti Vihar (Buddhist Temple)
- **Entry:** Free; donation ₹20.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: smooth stone floor, easy access. *Toddlers*: can sit on parents’ lap; the meditation hall is quiet.  
- **Best time:** Afternoon (2:00 pm – 4:00 pm) for a peaceful chant session.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Ashoka Pillar
- **Entry:** Free.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: short walk from parking, flat ground. *Toddlers*: safe to explore; keep away from the pillar’s base to avoid tripping.  
- **Best time:** Sunset (5:30 pm – 6:30 pm) for golden illumination.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

### Archaeological Museum (Sarnath)
- **Entry:** Included in Sarnath Museum ticket.  
- **Suitability:** Same as museum.  
- **Best time:** Same as museum.  

**Trust issue – Unlicensed guide trap:**  
- **Scam:** Street “tour guides” claim to be official and charge ₹300 – ₹500 for a “Sarnath package” that includes a fake ticket.  
- **Recommended approach:** Hire a guide through the Varanasi Tourist Information Centre (VIC) or use verified apps (e.g., *TripSathi*). Verify the guide’s ID badge.

**Sarnath food:**  
- **Signature dishes:** *Sarnath ki kachori* (spicy lentil filling), *Litti Chokha* (roasted wheat balls with mashed veggies), *Mithai* (peda, balushahi).  
- **For toddlers & young kids:** *Steamed rice with ghee*, *soft boiled potatoes*, *plain roti with a dab of ghee*.  
- **Vegetarian availability:** 100 % vegetarian; most stalls serve only veg fare.

## Ramnagar & Surroundings (West Bank)

**Key places:** Ramnagar Fort, Ramnagar Ghat, Vindhyachal Temple (nearby), Kaithi Sangam (hidden gem – see later)

### Ramnagar Fort
- **Entry:** ₹100 adult, ₹50 child (5‑12 yr).  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: uneven stone steps; use the ramp at the main gate. *Toddlers*: not ideal – the fort’s interior has narrow corridors and steep stairs.  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00 pm – 6:00 pm) for the best light on the palace walls.  
- **Seasonal closure:** Closed on Tuesdays and during major Hindu festivals (e.g., Navratri).

### Ramnagar Ghat
- **Entry:** Free.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: flat promenade, benches. *Toddlers*: safe to sit on the steps; avoid peak boat‑traffic times.  
- **Best time:** Evening (6:30 pm – 8:00 pm) for a quiet riverside walk.  
- **Seasonal closure:** None.

**Trust issue – Fort ticket overcharging:**  
- **Scam:** Some “private guides” ask for an extra “photo‑permit” of ₹200 – ₹300 on top of the official entry fee.  
- **Recommended approach:** Pay only at the ticket counter; request a receipt. If a guide insists, decline politely and walk in alone.

**Ramnagar food:**  
- **Signature dishes:** *Kachori*, *Jalebi*, *Aloo Tamatar Chaat*, *Makhana (fox nuts) fry*.  
- **For toddlers & young kids:** *Plain boiled rice*, *soft idli*, *steamed carrot‑peas mash*.  
- **Vegetarian availability:** High; most stalls are pure‑veg. Non‑veg (e.g., kebabs) is limited to a few roadside vendors.

## General Varanasi Notes

**Best time to visit:**  
- **October – March:** Cool (10‑25 °C), festivals (Diwali, Dev Deepawali) enhance cultural vibe; all attractions open.  
- **April – June:** Warm (30‑40 °C); early mornings are pleasant, but midday heat can be intense; boat rides still operate.  
- **July – September:** Monsoon; heavy rain can make ghats slippery and some outdoor events (e.g., Ganga Aarti) are postponed; however, crowds thin and hotel rates drop 20‑30 %.  

**Transport pricing:**  
- **Taxi/cab:** Metered fares start at ₹25 for the first km, then ₹12 / km. Airport‑to‑city centre typical ₹500‑₹650. Negotiate only for out‑station trips; insist on a meter for intra‑city rides.  
- **Auto‑rickshaw:** Flag‑down rate ₹15 for the first km, then ₹10 / km. Most drivers accept ₹200‑₹250 for a 5 km city tour; confirm price before boarding.  
- **App‑based options:** *Ola* and *Uber* operate in Varanasi; surge pricing peaks during festivals (up to 2×). *Rapido* (bike‑share) is cheap for solo travellers (₹30‑₹50 per short ride).  

**Dietary notes:**  
- **Dominant cuisine:** North‑Indian with a strong Awadhi influence; liberal use of ghee, mustard oil, and sometimes coconut oil (especially in sweets).  
- **Allergy risks:** Coconut‑based sweets (e.g., *Thandai*), peanuts in chaat, and occasional use of sesame oil.  
- **Vegetarian options:** Excellent; most street food is veg. Non‑veg (kebabs, fish fry) is limited to specific eateries; ask “shakahari” to ensure veg.

**Medical:**  
- **Nearest quality hospital:** *BHU Hospital (Institute of Medical Sciences)*, 3 km from the city centre, 24‑hour emergency, good cardiac and orthopaedic facilities.  
- **What to carry:** Basic first‑aid kit, any prescription meds, a copy of health insurance, and a small pack of oral rehydration salts (especially for monsoon heat.

## Getting There

**By train:**  
- **Nearest station:** Varanasi Junction (BSB), 2 km from the city centre.  
- **Key trains:** *Howrah–Varanasi Express* (12345, departs Delhi – Kanpur – Varanasi), *Delhi–Varanasi Rajdhani* (12430, premium service).  
- **Typical fare:** ₹800 – ₹2,200 (second class) from Delhi; ₹1,200 – ₹3,000 (AC 2‑Tier) from Kolkata.

**By air:**  
- **Nearest airport:** Lal Bahadur Shastri International Airport (VNS), 22 km from the city centre.  
- **Airlines:** IndiGo, Air India, SpiceJet, Vistara.  
- **One‑way fare (2025):** ₹4,000 – ₹12,000 from Delhi; ₹5,500 – ₹15,000 from Mumbai.

**By road:**  
- **Key highway:** NH 19 (formerly NH 2) connects Delhi, Kanpur, and Varanasi.  
- **Nearest state bus depot:** Varanasi Bus Stand (Madhya Pradesh), 3 km from the city centre.  
- **Inter‑city bus frequency:** Every 30 min on the Delhi‑Varanasi route; night buses available.  
- **Typical fare:** ₹800 – ₹1,200 for a non‑AC bus; ₹1,500 – ₹2,200 for a semi‑luxury AC coach.

## Hidden Gems

**Kaithi Sangam** (Confluence of Ganga, Varuna, and Assi)  
- **Why special:** Rarely crowded, offers a serene view of three rivers meeting; locals perform early‑morning rituals.  
- **Entry/cost:** Free.  
- **How to reach:** Auto‑rickshaw from Assi Ghat (≈ ₹80).  
- **Best time:** Dawn (5:30 am – 7:00 am) for misty reflections.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: flat walk, benches available. *Toddlers*: safe open space; keep child within sight.

**Silk Weaving Village – Madhurawati (near Varanasi)**  
- **Why special:** Live demonstration of Banarasi silk weaving, not listed in mainstream guides; chance to buy directly from artisans.  
- **Entry/cost:** ₹30 per person for a guided walk.  
- **How to reach:** Hire a local auto from Varanasi Railway Station (≈ ₹250).  
- **Best time:** Late morning (10:30 am – 12:30 pm) when looms are active.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: short walk, shaded lanes. *Toddlers*: can watch the colourful threads; avoid stepping on looms.

**Kachchi Dargah (Mughal‑Era Tomb)**  
- **Why special:** Quiet 17th‑century tomb with intricate stonework, overlooked by most tourists.  
- **Entry/cost:** Free; optional donation ₹20.  
- **How to reach:** Auto‑rickshaw from Ramnagar Fort (≈ ₹120).  
- **Best time:** Late afternoon (4:00 pm – 5:30 pm) for soft shadows.  
- **Suitability:** *Elderly*: uneven stones; use a walking stick. *Toddlers*: safe but keep close as the area is not fenced.

## Practical Tips

**Connectivity:**  
- Jio and Airtel provide strong 4G coverage across the city and ghats; **Vi** is patchy near the riverbanks, especially after monsoon. Download offline maps (Google Maps) before arrival.

**ATMs:**  
- Reliable ATMs: SBI (near BHU campus), HDFC (near Varanasi Junction), ICICI (near Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport).  
- Cash is preferred for street food and small‑shop purchases; most hotels accept cards, but a ₹2,000‑₹3,000 cash reserve is advisable.

**SIM cards:**  
- Purchase at the airport (Jio, Airtel kiosks) or authorized shops in the city centre (e.g., Jio Store on Chowk).  
- **Best coverage:** Jio for rural outskirts (e.g., Sarnath, Ramnagar); Airtel for urban core and ghats.  
- Bring passport copy, 2 × 2 cm photo, and ₹500‑₹1,000 for prepaid plan activation.
